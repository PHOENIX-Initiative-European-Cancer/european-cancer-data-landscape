### Registro Tumori Nazionale (ISS/Ministero)
- Law 29/2019 created the national network of cancer registries and the national registry; DM 1 Aug 2023 "Registro nazionale tumori" (23A04839, GU 203 of 31 Aug 2023, verified 2026-11-19 in the GU text) makes Ministero della Salute data controller and operator, defines tumours by ICD-9 140-239, ICD-10 C00-C97 and D00-D48 and "all editions and revisions" of ICD-O, lists data sources (regional SDO archives, hospital and IRCCS archives, pathology, mortality), requires Regions to name a regional reference centre within 180 days, prescribes annual bulk feeding by the regional centres with pseudonymised identifiers, and its Allegato A "Disciplinare tecnico" defines XML tracciati A (patient), B (case), C (additional variables), D (reference population). State-Regions agreement Atto Rep. 171 of 26 Jul 2023; Garante opinion n. 136 of 7 Apr 2022. Sources: https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/2023/08/31/203/sg/pdf (local copy italy-dl/gu203.pdf, pp. 18-40), https://www.normattiva.it/eli/id/2019/04/05/19G00036/ORIGINAL , https://www.statoregioni.it/media/6189/p-11-csr-atto-rep-n-171-26lug2023.pdf
- Implementation: PN REG "Specifiche funzionali dei tracciati" v2.2 (Jan 2025; versions since 15 Feb 2024), PNRR M6C2 sub-investment 1.3.2; annual XML feed from 21 regional reference centres (table 16: e.g. Piemonte AOU Città della Salute, Lombardia DG Welfare with 8 ATS registries, Veneto Azienda Zero, FVG CRO Aviano, Liguria San Martino, Emilia-Romagna IRST Meldola, Toscana ISPRO, Umbria Punto Zero, Lazio DEP ASL Roma 1, Campania Regione, Puglia AReSS, Basilicata CROB, Sicilia Assessorato, Sardegna ARES). FVG contributes since March 2024; "I numeri del cancro 2025" still calls the national registry "incompiuta". Sources: https://bura.regione.abruzzo.it/sites/bura.regione.abruzzo.it/files/bollettini/2026-09-01/allegato-4-pn-reg-registro-nazionale-tumori-specifiche-dei-tracciati.pdf , https://www.quotidianosanita.it/regioni-e-asl/fvg-il-registro-tumori-compie-anni/ , https://www.legatumori.mi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025_NDC_web.pdf
