Prepared 2026-11-18 for the ECCDM / PHOENIX editors. Companion to `italy-fhir.md`. "n/f" = not found. Evidence files in `italy-dl/`.

## C1. Cancer registries

### AIRTUM network
- **Accredited registries.** AIRTUM's registry selector lists 50 population registry areas (codes 02-51: Avellino, Bari, BAT, Basilicata, Benevento, Bergamo, Brescia, Brindisi, Caserta, Catania-Messina-Enna, Cosenza-Crotone, Cremona-Mantova, Ferrara, Foggia, Friuli Venezia Giulia, Latina, Lazio, Lecce, Liguria, Marche, Milano-Lodi, Modena, Monza Brianza-Lecco, Napoli 1/2/3, Nuoro, Palermo, Parma, Pavia, Piacenza, Piemonte, Ragusa-Caltanissetta, Reggio Calabria, Reggio Emilia, Romagna, Salerno, Sassari, Siracusa, Sondrio, Taranto, Toscana, Trapani-Agrigento, Trento, Umbria, Valle d'Aosta, Varese-Como, Veneto, Viterbo), plus Abruzzo and Molise "non accreditato", and 7 specialised registries (childhood: Campania, Marche, Piemonte; mesothelioma: Emilia-Romagna, Liguria; colorectal Modena; pancreas Reggio Emilia). The map page itself is dated Oct 2017 (70 %, 41.4 M covered; 28 % in activity; 2 % uncovered). Source: https://www.registri-tumori.it/cms/pagine/mappa-dei-registri-di-popolazione , https://www.registri-tumori.it/cms/pagine/mappa-dei-registri-specializzati-accreditati
- **Current coverage.** "I numeri del cancro 2024" used 35 registries covering >44 M people, about 80 % of residents; the March 2026 AIRTUM data presentation used 34 accredited population registries and >3 M incident cases. Sources: https://quifinanza.it/salute/tumori-numeri-italia-2024/871401/ , https://www.unipa.it/Il-prof.-Walter-Mazzucco-coordinatore-del-gruppo-di-lavoro-AIRTUM-ha-presentato-al-Ministero-della-Salute-i-dati-epidemiologici-sui-tumori-maligni-in-Italia/
- **Accreditation.** Documents required for accreditation and the data bank protocol (Sept 2020): tracciati A (incidence), B, C (optional additional variables), D (population), E (mortality); acceptance thresholds: 3 years of registration (5 if <500,000 pop.), DCO <3 %, MV 80-95 %, ill-defined sites <5 %, case count stability within 5 pp. Sources: https://www.registri-tumori.it/cms/system/files/DOCUMENTI_DA_INCLUDERE_ALLA_DOMANDA_DI_ACCREDITAMENTO.pdf , https://www.registri-tumori.it/cms/system/files/Protocollo%20Banca%20dati%20AIRTUM%202020.pdf
- **Record layout (AIRTUM 2020).** Fixed-width fields incl. ICDOT (ICD-O-3 topography Cxxx), ICDOM (morphology), GICDO grade, EDICDO (ICD-O edition), ICCC (derived for <=19 y), BASE of diagnosis, LATERAL, STADIOT/N/M (AJCC/UICC), TNM_COND (condensed TNM from SDO since 2018), EDITNM, INV_NEUR/VASC/LINF, CAUSA10 (ICD-10 cause of death). Source: same protocol, local copy italy-dl/airtum-protocollo2020.pdf
- **Software:** no national registry software found; registries use local systems (n/f for "Open Registry"/SEER tools in Italy).
- **Governance:** President Fabrizio Stracci (Università di Perugia, director Registro Tumori Umbria, ex ENCR steering committee); vice-presidents Silvia Francisci (ISS) and Gemma Gatta (INT Milano). Source: https://www.registri-tumori.it/cms/pagine/il-consiglio-direttivo
- **ECIS/ENCR:** Italian registries submit through the ENCR-JRC ECIS data call (latest deadline 31 Jan 2026; protocol updated 22 Jun 2026). Source: https://encr.eu/call-for-data , https://encr.eu/sites/default/files/Data_call/ECIS-data-protocol_20260622.pdf

### Registro Tumori Nazionale (ISS/Ministero)
- Law 29/2019 created the national network of cancer registries and the national registry; DM 1 Aug 2023 (GU 203, 31 Aug 2023) makes Ministero della Salute data controller and operator; State-Regions opinion Atto Rep. 171 of 26 Jul 2023. Sources: https://www.normattiva.it/eli/id/2019/04/05/19G00036/ORIGINAL , https://certifico.com/sicurezza-lavoro/news-sicurezza/decreto-1-agosto-2023 , https://www.statoregioni.it/media/6189/p-11-csr-atto-rep-n-171-26lug2023.pdf
- Implementation: PN REG "Specifiche funzionali dei tracciati" v2.2 (Jan 2025; versions since 15 Feb 2024), PNRR M6C2 sub-investment 1.3.2; annual XML feed from 21 regional reference centres (table 16: e.g. Piemonte AOU Città della Salute, Lombardia DG Welfare with 8 ATS registries, Veneto Azienda Zero, FVG CRO Aviano, Liguria San Martino, Emilia-Romagna IRST Meldola, Toscana ISPRO, Umbria Punto Zero, Lazio DEP ASL Roma 1, Campania Regione, Puglia AReSS, Basilicata CROB, Sicilia Assessorato, Sardegna ARES). FVG contributes since March 2024; "I numeri del cancro 2025" still calls the national registry "incompiuta". Sources: https://bura.regione.abruzzo.it/sites/bura.regione.abruzzo.it/files/bollettini/2026-09-01/allegato-4-pn-reg-registro-nazionale-tumori-specifiche-dei-tracciati.pdf , https://www.quotidianosanita.it/regioni-e-asl/fvg-il-registro-tumori-compie-anni/ , https://www.legatumori.mi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025_NDC_web.pdf
- Referto epidemiologico (art. 4 L. 29/2019): implementing decree n/f.

## C2. Screening and public datasets
- **ONS 2025 data** (published 23 Jul 2026): mammography uptake 53.1 % (North 63.1, South 40.0), extension 96.1 %; colorectal uptake 34.9 %; cervical 48.4 % (-2.6 pp); 40 % overall. Sources: https://www.quotidianosanita.it/studi-e-analisi/screening-oncologici-2025-pi-di-un-italiano-su-2-non-aderisce-migliora-la-mammografia-e-frena-lo-screening-cervicale-resta-il-divario-nord-sud/ , https://www.epicentro.iss.it/screening/aggiornamenti
- **"I numeri del cancro in Italia"** (AIOM, AIRTUM, Fondazione AIOM, ONS, PASSI, SIAPEC): 2025 edition, ~390,000 new cases estimated. Source: https://www.legatumori.mi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025_NDC_web.pdf
- **ISTAT mortality** tables (ICD-10): https://www.istat.it/tavole-di-dati/cause-di-morte/ ; Dryad dataset of cancer mortality rates 2009-2018: https://datadryad.org/stash/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.ns1rn8pvg
- **Ministry open data:** dati.salute.gov.it did not respond on 2026-11-18; SDO page with experimental ICD-10-IM tracciati: https://www.salute.gov.it/new/it/sistema-informativo/scheda-dimissione-ospedaliera-sdo/
- **Regional oncology data warehouses:** Emilia-Romagna DBO (database oncologico, linkable to SDO; drug-treatment coverage 31.2 % in 2022). Source: https://salute.regione.emilia-romagna.it/siseps/sanita/dbo/files/dbo_04_05_2023_pubbliche.pdf
- **ECIS:** Italian registries contribute via ENCR-JRC data calls (see C1).
- **Secondary use:** EDS (DM 31 Dec 2024) is the designated national secondary-use layer; HDAB not yet designated; EHDS Cabina di regia since May 2026 (see italy-fhir.md A3).

## C4. Non-FHIR digital
- **CDA-based FSE documents relevant to oncology:** Referto di Anatomia Patologica (RAP 1.4 schematron, FML map 1.6), Lettera di dimissione (LDO 5.5), Referto di radiologia (RAD 4.1), Referto specialistica (RSA 8.3), Lettera di invito per screening (1.2), Cartella Clinica Elettronica (CDA-CCE, approved Dec 2025); no PDTA document type found in the gateway catalogue. Source: https://github.com/ministero-salute/it-fse-catalogs
- **Structured pathology reporting:** Emilia-Romagna regional project "referto strutturato di anatomia patologica e biomolecolare" (2015) based on CAP synoptic checklists; no national SIAPEC-IAP synoptic standard found; SIAPEC congress: only 35 % of pathology labs fully digital. Sources in italy-fhir.md A2 and https://www.quotidianosanita.it/aiom/oncologia-in-umbria-foglietta-aiom-oggi-si-respira-unaria-di-nuova-collaborazione-molecular-tumor-board-integrazione-e-prevenzione/
