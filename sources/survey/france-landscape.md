# France: non-FHIR digital cancer landscape (survey for ECCDM / PHOENIX)

Compiled 2026-11-18 from web sources. Every bullet carries its source URL; "n/f" = not found in this survey. Companion report on the FHIR side: france-fhir (same scratchpad). Downloaded source texts: `scratchpad/survey/fr-nonfhir-dl/` and `scratchpad/dl-fr/`.

## 1. Cancer registries

### 1.1 Population-based registries (Réseau Francim)

- **Network size and coverage (2025)**: Francim groups 31 registries covering all adult cancers in 24 départements, specific cancers in 6 further départements, and childhood cancers nationwide. Francim "regroupe tous les registres des cancers évalués par le Comité d'Évaluation des Registres". https://www.francim-reseau.org/
- Panorama des cancers 2026 (INCa): "28 registres locaux couvrent environ 20 à 24 % de la population selon la localisation"; 2 national paediatric registries. https://www.cancer.fr/content/download/141527/file/PANOKFR2026_Panorama%20des%20cancers%202026.pdf?version=3
- Assemblée nationale report (2025) counts 33 registries: 19 general registries covering 24 départements, 12 specialised, 2 national; 24 % of population covered. https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/rapports/cion-soc/l17b1590_rapport-fond.pdf and https://www.vie-publique.fr/loi/289931-creation-dun-registre-national-des-cancers-loi-du-30-juin-2025
- Vidal (Jan 2026): "une trentaine de registres territoriaux (dont 27 subventionnés par l'Inca)", covering 20–24 % of the adult population. https://www.vidal.fr/actualites/37481-le-registre-national-des-cancers-un-nouvel-outil-pour-la-surveillance-epidemiologique-en-france.html

**General registries (all cancers), with start year, from the Francim registry list** https://www.francim-reseau.org/les-registres :
- Tarn (81) 1981; Hérault (34) 1986; Loire-Atlantique/Vendée (44, 85) 1998; Poitou-Charentes (16, 17, 79, 86) 2008; Lille et sa région (59) 2004; Doubs (25) 1976; Somme (80) 1982; Gironde (33) 2005 for all tumours (CNS tumours since 1999, haematological malignancies since 2002, run as several registries); Isère (38) 1978; Bas-Rhin (67) 1974; Haut-Rhin (68) 1988; Haute-Vienne (87) 1974; Calvados (14) general (non-digestive) 1978; Manche (50) 1994; La Réunion (974) 1988; Martinique (972) 1983; Guadeloupe (971) 2008; Guyane (973) 2005. **Governance and funding**
- Registries pool their data in a common database ("base commune") administered by Francim with Santé publique France and INCa and managed by the biostatistics department of the Hospices Civils de Lyon (HCL). Anonymised/aggregated data go to IARC (CI5), ENCR, EUROCARE, RARECARE, HAEMACARE, CONCORD. https://www.francim-reseau.org/
- Cancer surveillance rests on a four-party scientific partnership Francim / HCL / Santé publique France / INCa (programme de travail partenarial, 56 actions 2014–2019, indicators for >70 cancer types). https://odisse.santepubliquefrance.fr/explore/dataset/cancer-incidence-observee-en-zone-registres-departement ; https://www.santepubliquefrance.fr/docs/article/un-programme-de-travail-partenarial-pour-lepidemiologie-des-cancers-bilan-2014-2019
- Registries are funded by the State via SpF and INCa, plus CHU, CLCC, ARS and local authorities; research by ANR, INCa, IReSP, Ligue, ARC. https://www.francim-reseau.org/les-registres
- Registries are qualified by the Comité d'évaluation des registres (CER; SpF, Inserm, INCa); a 2026 call for qualified personalities closed 29 March 2026. https://santepubliquefrance.fr/comite-d-evaluation-des-registres
- **ENCR/IACR**: Francim is listed as an ENCR member entity (director Dr Alain Monnereau) https://encr.eu/node/382 ; individual French registries are ENCR members (e.g. Doubs, Gironde haematological, Tarn; Dr Laetitia Daubisse-Marliac of the Tarn registry sits on the ENCR Steering Committee) https://encr.eu/encr-members-contact-list?page=2 ; https://encr.eu/steeringcommittee . French registries contributing to ECIS historical data include Calvados, Gironde, Lille area, Limousin, Loire-Atlantique/Vendée, Manche, Poitou-Charentes, Somme, Tarn. https://ecis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/list-registries-contributing-ecis-historical-data . Full French IACR membership list: n/f (IACR hosts ICD-O-3.2 files at https://www.iacr.com.fr/ ).

**Base commune: data items and format**
- Coding standard: data coded in CIM-O-3 (ICD-O-3); national estimates files published with a variable dictionary ("fichier readme", Table_sites.txt). https://cancer.fr/Media/documents/observation-et-documentation/incidence_estimations-nationales_fichier-readme_2018
- The CNIL opinion describes the base commune as "un registre commun afin de centraliser les données des registres locaux participant au réseau FRANCIM" and notes the decree imposes annual transmission of local registry data to it; the CNIL questioned keeping a base commune separate from the national registry. https://kohenavocats.fr/?p=1792011 (reproduces Délibération CNIL n° 2025-118 of 11 Dec 2025)
- INCa designation dossier (volet 2, 2026) refers to "cas incidents envoyés à la base commune" as the evaluation unit. https://www.cancer.fr/Media/documents/registre-national-des-cancers/volet-2-dossier-evaluation-du-dossier-de-candidature-pour-la-designation-et-l-evaluation-des-organismes-mettant-en-aeuvre-des-registres-locaux-des
- Published variable dictionary, file format (CSV/fixed-width), ICD-O-3 edition (3.1 vs 3.2), TNM edition and multiple-primary rules used by the base commune: **n/f** on public web pages (Francim site has no technical documentation; /le-reseau and /la-base-commune are 404). ICD-O-3.2 is the IACR/ENCR reference for incidence from 2020 (Swiss practice) https://www.ge.ch/document/39714/telecharger ; IARC minimum dataset described at https://publications.iarc.who.int/_publications/media/download/3544/2937cb81e27141fcf82e391417b6eba2ca56adce.pdf

### 1.2 Registre national des cancers (RNC), created 2025

- **Law**: Loi n° 2025-596 du 30 juin 2025 (JO 1 July 2025, texte 3) creates the RNC and entrusts it to INCa (article L. 1415-2-1 CSP); unanimous vote. https://www.vie-publique.fr/loi/289931-creation-dun-registre-national-des-cancers-loi-du-30-juin-2025 ; https://lcp.fr/index%2Ephp/actualites/creation-d-un-registre-national-des-cancers-la-proposition-de-loi-votee-a-l-unanimite-a
- **Decree**: Décret n° 2025-1366 du 26 décembre 2025 (JO 28 Dec 2025, texte 55), in force 1 Jan 2026; sets sources, pseudonymisation and matching conditions, articulation with local registries, modifies the NIR framework decree 2019-341. https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/decret-n-2025-1366-du-26-decembre-2025-portant-creation-du-traitement-denomme-registre-national-des-cancers-prevu-a-larticle-l-1415-2-1-du-code-de-la-sante-publique/ ; https://www.vie-publique.fr/en-bref/301572-sante-creation-du-registre-national-des-cancers
- **Scope / population**: persons entering a prevention or screening pathway, suspected of cancer, receiving cancer care, or who are or have been affected. Three aims: national surveillance, follow-up of care pathways, research data warehouse. https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-8571.pdf (p. 38-39)
- **Data sources (decree, as reported by INCa webinar 21 Jan 2026 via Vidal)**: eight sources in two groups. Single producers: SNDS "cohorte cancer"; CépiDc-Inserm causes of death; OSCOUR emergency-department network (SpF); CNSA medico-social base. Multiple producers: CRCDC screening information systems (breast, colorectal, cervical); territorial registries; hospital and laboratory information systems "incluant les comptes rendus de RCP, les PPS, les données d'anatomopathologie, les comptes rendus de génétique moléculaire". Plus, for specific approved projects: cohorts, EDS, clinico-biological bases, industry data. Transfer is "annuel, gratuit et obligatoire". https://www.vidal.fr/actualites/37481-le-registre-national-des-cancers-un-nouvel-outil-pour-la-surveillance-epidemiologique-en-france.html
- Senate report counts nine sources: five "qualifying" (SNDS, local registries, hospital/lab IS, screening actors, regional specific IS i.e. DCC) and four for follow-up (OSCOUR, CépiDc, CNSA, re-integration of studies). INCa gets NIR access for deterministic matching. https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-8571.pdf
- **Expected variables / qualification**: case confirmed, dated and qualified by "localisation du cancer, comportement invasif ou in situ, type histologique, stade au diagnostic"; registry data are the gold standard; qualification algorithms validated on registry-covered zones then applied nationally; AI methods to be tested. https://www.vidal.fr/actualites/37481-le-registre-national-des-cancers-un-nouvel-outil-pour-la-surveillance-epidemiologique-en-france.html
- **CNIL opinion (Délib. 2025-118, 11 Dec 2025)**: criticises collection of full birth date and names; names only for NIR reconstitution; matching to be done by INCa after pseudonymisation in a segregated integration environment; SNDS rules apply (arrêté 6 May 2024 security referential); retention of identifying data 24 months, pseudonymised data 50 years questioned; a scientific and ethics committee (art. R. 1415-2-5) gives opinions on reuse; hosting by HDS-certified providers. https://kohenavocats.fr/?p=1792011 ; CNIL agenda https://cnil.fr/fr/ordre-du-jour-de-la-seance-pleniere-du-11-decembre-2025
- **Retention / access (INCa webinar)**: identifying data kept max 24 months in a high-security environment, pseudonymised data for the last 20 years; ~6 months from protocol to data provision; data lag ~2 years; insurers get only pseudonymised sets. https://www.vidal.fr/actualites/37481-le-registre-national-des-cancers-un-nouvel-outil-pour-la-surveillance-epidemiologique-en-france.html
- **Timeline and status (Senate, July 2026)**: cohorte cancer since 2011 (annual CNAM extraction, 13.6 M persons 2010–2023); PDC 2019–2023; RNC data warehouse homologated 9 April 2026 for 3 years; first deterministic match SNDS cohort × local registries (adult solid tumours 2010–2021) with 95.3 % success; extension to adult haematological then paediatric cancers under way; 35 research projects approved; public open-data portal with aggregated data planned H2 2026; governance = comité scientifique et éthique, comité des partenaires, comité de pilotage. https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-8571.pdf
- **Cost**: 58.2 M€ 2025–2028 (36.7 M€ for piloting local registries, 2.1 M€ clinical/biological data collection, 0.7 M€ screening data, 3 M€ integration/matching/pseudonymisation, 8.1 M€ data provision, 7.7 M€ staff); 11.1 M€ new money; 13 ETPT + 5 CDD. https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-857-syn.pdf ; https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-8571.pdf
- **Local registry designation**: procedure voted by INCa board 16 March 2026; two-part application (volet 1 deadline 15 April 2026); a common information system for local registries is being built. https://www.cancer.fr/Media/documents/registre-national-des-cancers/volet-1-demande-de-designation-du-dossier-de-candidature-pour-la-designation-et-l-evaluation-des-organismes-mettant-en-oeuvre-les-registres-locau ; https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-8571.pdf
- **INCa–Health Data Hub framework agreement (4 Sept 2026, 4 years)**: PDS support for the pseudonymisation circuit (via "Mon interface d'appariement", by 31 Dec 2027); RNC metadata into the national metadata catalogue from 1 Jan 2027; hosting of RNC IS on PDS infrastructure studied from 1 April 2027; transmission of the SNDS "cohorte cancer" to be set in a joint-controllership convention; joint work on synthetic data, AI building blocks and "retour d'expériences sur les modèles de standard de données appliqués à la cancérologie en vue de favoriser la structuration et l'interopérabilité". Signed by Suzette Delaloge (INCa president) and Hela Ghariani (PDS DG). No FHIR/CDA/OMOP named. https://health-data-hub.fr/sites/default/files/2026-09/4_09_26%20Signature%20accord%20cadre%20INCa_PDS.pdf ; https://health-data-hub.fr/sites/default/files/2026-09/CP%20Signature%20accord%20cadre%20INCa_PDS_04092026.pdf
- **Transmission format / standards named in decree or INCa documents**: n/f (the Senate recommends an urgent ministerial instruction on "modalités de transmission à l'INCa des données"). https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-857-syn.pdf . FHIR/CDA/XML mention in decree: none found. The CI-SIS FRCP volet's section 'FR-Conclusion-du-CR-ACP' and PPS CDA are the only standardised clinical documents among the named sources (see §4).
- Project lead at INCa: Claire Morgand, directrice de l'observation, des sciences des données et de l'évaluation. https://www.vidal.fr/actualites/37481-le-registre-national-des-cancers-un-nouvel-outil-pour-la-surveillance-epidemiologique-en-france.html

### 1.4 Hospital-based and clinical registries / cohorts
- **ESME (Unicancer, since 2014)**: real-world data warehouse of CLCC patients (breast, lung, ovary …), CNIL authorised 2019/2020, >600 variables, 80+ academic projects; in the HDH catalogue. https://www.unicancer.fr/en/programs/esme/ ; https://www.oncopacacorse.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/C.Jouffroy-Zeller-Unicancer-Cohortes-de-donnees-en-vie-reelle-ARC2026.pdf ; https://senat.fr/rap/r22-873/r22-8732.html
- **Harmonie (Unicancer, launched 22 Sept 2025)**: new EDS linking Unicancer real-world programmes with SNDS for >3 M breast/lung/ovarian cancer patients since 2008, updated yearly to 2030; CNIL authorised. https://www.unicancer.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/communique-de-presse-harmonie.pdf
- **ConSoRe (Unicancer, 2013)**: multisite search engine over CLCC records; **UNIBASE**: HDH/Unicancer/Ligue project for the 10 CLCC with ConSoRe. https://www.health-data-hub.fr/sites/default/files/2021-11/CP%20UNIBASE.pdf ; https://unicancer.fr/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/newsletterdata_024-vf.pdf
- **Onco-Data-Hub (ODH, Unicancer/Roche)** listed in the EMA RWD catalogue with SNDS linkage (probabilistic) from H2 2025. https://catalogues.ema.europa.eu/node/4354/data-flows-and-management
- **CANTO (NCT01993498)**: Unicancer prospective cohort of 12 012 women with stage I–III breast cancer, 26 centres, PI Fabrice André (Gustave Roussy). https://www.unicancer.fr/en/programs/canto/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6735667
- **CONSTANCES** (Inserm/CNAM, ~200 000 adults) linked to SNIIRAM/PMSI/CNAV/CépiDc; 10 232 participants in the PFMG2025 POPGEN pilot. https://epidemiologie-france.aviesan.fr/epidemiology/records/cohorte-des-consultants-des-centres-d-examens-de-sante/eng-gb ; https://pfmg2025.aviesan.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Presentation-PFMG2025-CAD_colloque-SHS_29-Juin-2023.pdf
- **E3N-Générations** (ex E3N/E4N, ~140 000 participants, MGEN women since 1990). https://www.e3n-generations.fr/sites/default/files/2026-04/Bulletin_E3N_GENERATIONS_N13_mars2026.pdf
- **Rare-cancer networks (INCa)**: 23 clinical networks with national clinico-biological databases in 17 of them (28 500 cases by 2015), e.g. NETSARC (sarcomas, labelled 2009); 2025 re-labelling call. https://www.cancer.fr/content/download/7457/file/Resaux-nationaux-cancers-rares-adultes-2015.pdf?version=7 ; https://en-www.cancer.fr/content/download/97709/file/AAC+2025+Labellisation+r%C3%A9seaux+cancers+rares_Application+file.docx?version=2
- ARC, CRISP, ELCC: n/f as French cancer registries.

## 3. Cancer infrastructure

- **INCa**: GIP created 2004, dual ministry tutelage; budget 125.4 M€ in 2025 (63 % research), up from 85 M€ in 2019; missions include research, observation/epidemiology, prevention/screening, care organisation, and since 2025 the RNC. https://www.senat.fr/rap/r25-857/r25-857-syn.pdf
- **Stratégie décennale 2021–2030**: roadmap 1 (2021–2025, 237 actions, ~90 % started); roadmap 2 (2026–2030) launched 4 Feb 2026, >1.7 bn €, 5 priorities. https://www.cancer.fr/content/download/133816/file/FR2STRAT26_Feuille%20de%20route%202026-2030.pdf?version=2 ; https://www.info.gouv.fr/actualite/la-nouvelle-feuille-de-route-contre-les-cancers ; bilan 2021–2025 https://www.cancer.fr/content/download/132358/file/RAPFR1STRAT25_Rapport_feuilleroute_2125_pages.pdf?version=2
- **SIRIC** (3rd wave, results 26 Jan 2023, 5 years 2023–2027, 8 sites): CARPEM (AP-HP), CURAMUS (AP-HP/Sorbonne), InsiTu (AP-HP, new), EpiCURE (Gustave Roussy), ILIAD (Nantes/Angers), LYriCAN+ (Lyon), Montpellier Cancer, Curie. https://siric.institut-curie.org/page/siric-label ; https://www.centreleonberard.fr/sites/default/files/2023-02/2023-02-cp-lyrican-siric-final.pdf ; https://www.aphp.fr/actualites/trois-sites-de-recherche-integree-sur-le-cancer-siric-de-lap-hp-labellises-par-linstitut

## Implications for a European cancer data model

France is pivoting from a partial, registry-centred system (Francim, 20–24 % population, ICD-O-3-coded base commune at HCL) to a nationwide, multi-source, matched registry run by INCa (RNC, in force 1 Jan 2026, 95 % deterministic SNDS×registry match achieved, open portal due H2 2026) whose declared qualifying variables are exactly the ECCDM core (topography, behaviour, morphology, stage at diagnosis, dates, treatments, vital status) and whose named feeder documents are the CI-SIS CDA R2 documents (fiche RCP with CIM-10 tumour, CIM-O morphology, TNM with mandatory edition, ECOG, phase, treatment type; PPS; CR-ACP conclusion with ADICAP) rather than FHIR. An ECCDM-to-France mapping should therefore target (a) the Francim/ENCR-JRC registry variables and ICD-O-3.2 behaviour/morphology rules, (b) the FRCP/PPS CDA value sets (jdv-*-cisis OIDs) and ADICAP, (c) SNDS/PMSI codes (CIM-10, CCAM, ALD) that the RNC uses to qualify cases, and (d) OMOP via the DARWIN EU/EHDEN French partners (Bordeaux, AP-HP, AP-HM, HDH SNDS). No French document names FHIR for registry transmission; the INCa–HDH agreement only commits to "retour d'expériences sur les modèles de standard de données appliqués à la cancérologie", which is the natural entry point for proposing ECCDM, with INCa (OSIRIS owner and EUnetCCC coordinator), the HDH (TEHDAS2/EHDS) and ANS (CI-SIS) as the three counterparts.
