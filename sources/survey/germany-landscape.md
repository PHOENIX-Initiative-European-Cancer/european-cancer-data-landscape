# Germany deep dive, part 2: registries, datasets, infrastructure, non-FHIR digital health, funding, people

For the editors of the HL7 Europe Common Cancer Data Model (ECCDM) and the PHOENIX Initiative. Status 2026-11-18/19. Deepens the Germany section of `dach-benelux.md` and `dach-benelux-landscape.md`; companion file `germany-fhir.md` covers FHIR specifications, terminologies and FHIR people. Every bullet ends with its source; "n/f" = not found in a citable source.

Headline findings:
- 15 clinical cancer registries for 16 Länder (Berlin and Brandenburg share one), coordinated by the Plattform § 65c; all accept oBDS XML v3 (production schema versions 3.0.1–3.0.4 per product; 3.0.5 not yet in Hessen's list), 40 vendor interfaces accepted; uniform Meldevergütung since 01.02.2024 (diagnosis 19.50 €).
- The national dataset at ZfKD (oBDS_v3.0.4_RKI, >120 variables, clinical data since diagnosis year 2020) is delivered yearly and is not linkable to external data; its schema is open on GitHub, access by application under § 5 BKRG. The IGES evaluation of clinical cancer registration (Oct 2026) found benefits "deutlich hinter den Erwartungen"; AI-CARE harmonised >1 million patients across 14 registries in 2026.
- Research access is shifting to the GDNG stack (FDZ Gesundheit, Datenzugangs- und Koordinierungsstelle at BfArM, ePA research data) and to MII/NUM (FDPG with 41 university hospitals; NUM-DIZ from 2027), while the § 64e genome model project runs its own BfArM platform (25,000 datasets by June 2026).
- Non-FHIR reality: tumour documentation runs on ONKOSTAR, GTDS, CREDOS and ~40 other systems; openEHR is used for oBDS in HiGHmed/Lübeck; pathology reporting and E-Rezept for cytostatics remain unstructured or outside the TI; four DiGA have oncology indications.
- Funding in 2026/2027: Nationale Dekade gegen Krebs calls (e2Health open to 30.06.2027), G-BA Innovationsfonds NVF short call open to 31.12.2026, Deutsche Krebshilfe Translationale Onkologie LOI 09.12.2026, MV § 64e third period applications closed 18.09.2026, NUM 3.0 to 2030, MII funding ends 2026.
