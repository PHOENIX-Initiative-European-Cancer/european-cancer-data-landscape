### Message types and business logic
- Three bundle types distinguished by `meta.tag` from an IKNL `processing-target` code system: `NKR_ODIA` "Incidentie bericht" (patient, organisation, encounter diagnosis, problem-list conditions), `NKR_GBS` "Bericht voor verrichting" (adds EpisodeOfCare, Procedure, ServiceRequest, lab result; a `gbs-med` variant carries medication; `gbs-opt-out` carries an opt-out patient), `HOV155` (organisation plus NcrEhrDataDocument and NcrEhrSchemaDocument, i.e. the HOVON155 haemato-oncology trial dataset delivered as embedded JSON). https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/tree/develop/input/fsh/instances
- Patient selection by ICD-10: C00-C97, D01-D09, D32-D33, D35.2, D42-D43, D45-D47, E85.4, E85.8, E85.9, and all M-codes (morphology). Trigger is a new diagnosis from this list, sent 28 days after first recording to filter false-positive suspicions; daily bundles thereafter for new items (each surgical procedure; medication and radiotherapy triggers "to be added"). https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/blob/develop/input/pagecontent/Selectieenabstractieepddata.md
- Patient opt-out for research must suppress transmission, retroactively. Patients are matched across hospitals by name, postcode, birth date and tumour data. Same source.
- Transport: FHIR R4 JSON bundles over a VPN tunnel to the IKNL VPN gateway into a private IKNL FHIR server; onboarding via fhir@iknl.nl; error reports to meldingen.fhir@iknl.nl. https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/blob/develop/input/pagecontent/Requirements.md
- Disclaimer in the IG: inclusion/exclusion value sets are still under development, so filtering happens at the source; per-item value sets will be added in later versions. https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/blob/develop/input/pagecontent/businesslogics.md

### NKR item sets covered
- The IG targets the items that are "often recorded structured and as far as possible part of the BgZ" out of the NKR item sets; the full NKR item-set catalogue (dated 2025-06-01) lists about 45 tumour-specific sets in eight groups: solid tumours (breast, bone and soft-tissue sarcoma, head and neck, lung, mesothelioma, thymoma, NET/NEC, thyroid, parathyroid), gynaecological (ovary, endometrium, cervix, vulva), digestive (oesophagus/stomach, small bowel, colorectal, anus, pancreas, HCC, biliary), paediatric (bone sarcoma, hepatoblastoma, leukaemia, lymphoma, nephroblastoma, neuroblastoma, CNS), neuro-oncology (glioma, meningioma, pituitary), skin (BCC, SCC, melanoma), urogenital (prostate, bladder, kidney, renal pelvis/ureter, urethra, testis, penis) and one haemato-oncology set. https://iknl.nl/nkr/registratie/itemsets
- "Basis" dataset: in 2025 IKNL fixed a per-tumour "kerndataset" with a "flexibele schil" of items registered on a smaller population; 2026 goals are to finalise the core dataset and to harmonise the item sets of two tumour types between the NKR and DICA audits. IKNL Jaarplan 2026, p. 20-21, https://iknl.nl/getmedia/42bb9bd9-ac2b-4f95-9ed6-a6de9e830b0d/Jaarplan-2026.pdf
- DICA audit item sets delivered by IKNL from the NKR: 2026 DCRA C and D set https://iknl.nl/getmedia/c1f5b0a4-c65b-4bf5-b46a-080c026ad01f/2026-Itemset-DCRA-C-en-D-set.pdf ; 2025 NBCA D set https://iknl.nl/getmedia/7372ea58-88b9-48f6-a334-3650dffd0834/2025-Itemset-NBCA-D-set.pdf

### Extraction architecture ("NKR-EHR" pipeline)
- R(H)ONDA (Realtime (Hemato-)ONcology Data): the automated stream went live mid-2024 from eight hospitals with a Performation "Datagateway", delivering basic patient, institution and diagnosis data (phase 1) near real-time, with eight more hospitals following; in July 2025 15 institutions were delivering; 2026 goal is to scale items and hospitals. The delivery "follows the FHIR standard". IKNL Jaarverslag 2024 p. 23 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/5e2b2905-4cf4-465c-985f-7eaa037febee/IKNL-Jaarverslag-2024.pdf ; Jaarplan 2026 p. 21.
- PLUGIN pilot with Radboudumc: the hospital exposes EHR data in FHIR, selected automatically via vantage6 (federated, data stays local); 13 items transferred successfully in the pilot; a data manager otherwise spends about 5.25 hours per patient on quality data. https://www.oncologie.nu/nieuws/plugin-pilot-iknl-haalt-realtime-data-uit-systeem-radboudumc/ ; Jaarverslag 2024 p. 23.
- Pathology feed: automated reading of PALGA protocol data since 2022 (breast protocol MammacarcinoomTotaal), 2023 three melanoma protocols plus ColonRectaalCarcinoom, 2024 cervix protocol; limits: many remaining protocols hold few NKR items, not all labs use protocols, two labs have not consented. Jaarverslag 2024 p. 14-15.
- LBZ (DHD) link: a generic person-level linkage between the Landelijke Basisregistratie Ziekenhuiszorg and the NKR was realised in 2024; the discharge date is read automatically from the LBZ; an algorithm signals metastatic breast cancer from LBZ data. Jaarplan 2026 p. 21.
- EHR vendors: no public IKNL document names Epic, ChipSoft HiX or Nexus connectors (n/f). Market context: ChipSoft about 71 percent, Epic 14 percent, Nexus 11 percent of Dutch hospital EHRs, with Cerner leaving the market in 2026. https://www.consultancy.nl/nieuws/51326/epd-markt-voor-ziekenhuizen-in-handen-van-drie-partijen
- Automation volumes: more than half of new NKR diagnoses are processed fully automatically (mostly skin cancers from pathology); about 200,000 tumours automatic and 130,000 partly manual expected in 2026. Jaarplan 2026 p. 19.
