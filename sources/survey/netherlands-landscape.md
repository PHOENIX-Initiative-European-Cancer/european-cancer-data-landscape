Prepared 2026-11-18 for the ECCDM editors and the PHOENIX Initiative. Companion to `netherlands-fhir.md`; deepens, does not repeat, the baseline in `dach-benelux.md` and `dach-benelux-landscape.md`. "n/f" = searched, not found.

## NKR (Nederlandse Kankerregistratie), IKNL
- National coverage since 1989; the only oncology hospital registry in the Netherlands with data on all cancer patients; codes TNM (UICC), ICD-O-3 topography and morphology, comorbidity at diagnosis and initial treatment; data used in more than 200 peer-reviewed publications a year; linked to IARC and ENCR, participates in EUROCARE. https://iknl.nl/en/ncr . ENCR member entry (members contact list read 2026-11-19): the Netherlands has one member, "Netherlands Cancer Registry", population 16,900,000 (year 2015), director Dr. Otto Visser. https://www.encr.eu/encr-members-contact-list (saved at scratchpad/verify/encr-members-2026-11-19.txt)
- Data sources and signalling: PALGA electronic signalling and protocol data since 2016 (protocol feed since 2022); LBZ hospital discharge data via DHD (person-level linkage realised 2024, discharge date auto-filled); Performation Datagateway from hospital EHRs (R(H)ONDA, 15 hospitals July 2025); CBS for vital status and causes of death; BRP death status under a RvIG authorisation decree. IKNL Jaarverslag 2024 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/5e2b2905-4cf4-465c-985f-7eaa037febee/IKNL-Jaarverslag-2024.pdf ; Jaarplan 2026 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/42bb9bd9-ac2b-4f95-9ed6-a6de9e830b0d/Jaarplan-2026.pdf ; BRP decree https://publicaties-prod.rvig.nl/sites/default/files/Besluit/Onderzoeksinstellingen/605301%20Integraal%20Kankercentrum%20Nederland%20%28IKNL%29.pdf
- Volumes and timeliness (Jaarplan 2026): about 200,000 tumours registered automatically from pathology in 2026 (skin cancers incl. melanoma with Breslow 0.8 mm or less since 2024) and about 130,000 partly manual; target registration within one year of diagnosis with fewer than 3,000 (2 percent) late; follow-up "vervolgepisodes" (recurrence, progression, standardised definitions since 2014) 15,000 to 20,000 a year in 2017-2023, target 25,000 in 2026 with LBZ-based signalling algorithms for at least two more tumour types; 10 to 20 externally funded additional registration projects a year with 5 to 10 FTE.
- Registration application RANK (more than ten years old) is being rebuilt from 2025 with internal and external capacity. RANK file layouts per tumour type (2025 edition) https://iknl.nl/getmedia/b7276fdb-be51-4f1d-aa3a-e20311af8c4f/RANK-Bestandsindeling-tumorsoort-2025.pdf
- Item sets per tumour type (about 45, list dated 2025-06-01) https://iknl.nl/nkr/registratie/itemsets ; per-tumour "kerndataset" plus "flexibele schil" fixed in 2025, to be finalised in 2026.
- Open data: "NKR Cijfers" interactive incidence, prevalence, survival and mortality tables https://iknl.nl/nkr-cijfers ; "NKR Online" (registered-user portal, menu item) and the "Nederlandse Kankeratlas" (with Cancer Council Queensland method) https://iknl.nl/en/ncr ; NKR data catalogue per cancer type incl. OMOP-CDM availability (Jaarplan 2026 p. 32); synthetic breast cancer dataset (also as OMOP database) https://iknl.nl/getmedia/07f1656b-5189-43ba-a62c-5daa4049170f/aanvraag-synthetische-dataset-2024.pdf ; https://github.com/IKNL/synthetic-breast-cancer-OMOP-database
- Data request procedure: only statistics or scientific research; standard deliveries exclude identifying, hospital-identifying data and exact dates; research requests need a protocol, 150-word rationale, 50-word NKR-necessity statement, funding section and per-variable justification; review by a scientific committee and the supervisory committee on the registry; download link by e-mail after approval; contact gegevensaanvraag@iknl.nl. Checklist Nov 2025 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/c11bc527-d536-4275-8b55-fb8ad3abc7c0/Checklist-bij-NKR-gegevensaanvraag-IKNL-november-2025.pdf ; infographic https://iknl.nl/getmedia/7f89d286-58f0-49ae-8c63-1e0b60932f09/IKNL-infographic-gegevensaanvraag-NKR.pdf ; scoring form https://iknl.nl/getmedia/ee18cb4c-9cdf-4d08-9485-17443785debc/Wetenschappelijke-toetsing-beoordelingsformulier-uniform.pdf
- Standing linkages offered: CBS, PALGA, PACAP (pancreas), PLCRC (colorectal), POCOP (oesophago-gastric), PROFIEL (PROMs); IZA network tool and regional reports. Menu at https://iknl.nl/nkr/registratie/itemsets
- Legal basis: a trajectory towards a statutory basis for the NKR was a supervisory-board topic in 2024 (Jaarverslag 2024, section 3.3). IKNL's trial office is being transferred to the NFU in 2026 (Jaarplan 2026 p. 26).
- International: NKR is a DARWIN EU data partner (EMA list dated 2026-02-16, "Netherlands Cancer Registry (NCR) - Integraal Kankercentrum Nederland") https://www.ema.europa.eu/system/files/documents/other/data-partners-onboarded-darwin-eu-phase-i-ii-iii-iv_en.pdf ; EHDEN data partner with NCR in OMOP in the EHDEN catalogue https://iknl.nl/en/news/ncr-data-in-omop-cdm ; delivers incidence and survival to ECIS; JRC cooperation agreement March 2024; MoU with Health-RI spring 2024; co-leads the Dutch cancer data node with Health-RI.
- Standard texts for publications using NKR data (March 2026) https://iknl.nl/getmedia/33117624-693d-447c-9cf0-b86ccd3a275f/Standaardteksten-publicaties-NKR-data.pdf

## PALGA (pathology registry)
- Stichting PALGA founded 1971; national network since 1991 covering all Dutch pathology labs (64 labs, about 42 million records on almost 10 million patients, 40 to 60 data requests a year, figures from 2007); the only body in the Netherlands collecting, protecting and providing pathology data; funded by VWS. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4618410 ; https://www.palga.nl/ ; https://www.oncologie.nu/nieuws/vijftig-jaar-palga-een-uniek-netwerk-met-oog-voor-de-toekomst/
- Governance of data release: Wetenschappelijke Raad advises on scientific merit, Privacy Commissie on privacy (privacy regulation v1.2, 2012). https://www.vzinfo.nl/sites/default/files/2021-11/privacyreglement_pidb_v1.2_vastgesteld_6nov2012.pdf ; current researcher page timed out on 2026-11-18 (https://www.palga.nl/onderzoekers/), current fees and turnaround: n/f (re-checked 2026-11-19: palga.nl pages time out for automated fetch; needs browser). Governance per oncologie.nu: director dr. Aletta Debernardi, board chair prof. dr. Iris Nagtegaal; all Dutch pathology labs are connected and the archive held almost 80 million records at the 50-year mark. https://oncologie.nu/nieuws/de-uitgestoken-hand-van-palga ; https://www.oncologie.nu/nieuws/vijftig-jaar-palga-een-uniek-netwerk-met-oog-voor-de-toekomst/ (verified 2026-11-19).
- Protocol governance: PALGA protocol working group backed by the PALGA board and NVVP; all national protocols approved by the NVVP committee. https://richtlijnendatabase.nl/richtlijn/borstkanker/pathologie.html
- Pathology Image Exchange platform operational. https://oncologie.nu/nieuws/platform-pathology-image-exchange-sinds-kort-operationeel ; PALGA and Health-RI work with cBioPortal for molecular MDO data (2023). https://www.oncologie.nu/nieuws/spotify-voor-medische-data/

## Health-RI, HDAB, Landelijk Dekkend Netwerk
- Health-RI: national health data infrastructure; Nationaal Groeifonds award €69 million in two phases (€22 million conditional in April 2021, €47 million reserved and released by the cabinet in 2022/2023). https://rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2022/02/NGFHR2201_bewerkt_11-02-2022.pdf ; https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/news/221565/kabinet-trekt-flink-bedrag-uit-voor-vijf-nationaal-groeifonds-projecten ; RRF contribution €22 million https://commission.europa.eu/projects/health-research-infrastructure_de
- Roles: part of the consortium designing the Dutch HDAB; national health data catalogue (DCAT) as EHDS entry point; Landelijk Dekkend Netwerk supplies standardised datasets with oncology as a planned use case; OMOP named as the research data model in its architecture. https://health-ri.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/FSD/pages/782172162/FAQ ; https://www.oncologie.nu/nieuws/de-nederlandse-zorg-klaarstomen-voor-de-ehds/ ; https://health-ri.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/HNG/pages/608313194
- EU: Dutch lead for the Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI, 1+MG), leads GDI WP6 data management; coordinates the EU project CANDLE on cancer data nodes; consortium partner in ECHoS with IKNL and NFK. https://health-ri.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/HNG/pages/608312395 ; https://eatris.eu/?p=43110 ; https://iknl.nl/en/news/echos-2
- IKNL and Health-RI co-lead the "Data-knooppunt Oncologie" (Dutch cancer data node). Jaarverslag 2024 p. 25.
- ELSI Servicedesk (not "ELSA"): launched 2018, coordinated from NKI, initiated by BBMRI-NL and Federa-COREON, now a Health-RI service with a tiered model (self-service FAQ, templates, tailored support). https://www.nki.nl/news-events/news/launch-national-servicedesk/ ; https://elixir-europe.github.io/ds-handbook/ELSI-servicedesk .
