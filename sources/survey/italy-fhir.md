## A1. National FHIR: HL7 Italia base, IT Core, FSE 2.0, EDS, DGC

### HL7 Italia base IG (legacy) and IT Core (successor)
- **hl7.fhir.it.base** stays at 0.1.0 (Public Comment ballot, 2020-06-15) in its package-list; the CI build is 0.1.1 draft, FHIR 4.0.1, and depends on `hl7.fhir.it.terminology` "current". The repo was last touched 2026-03-24. Sources: https://hl7.it/fhir/base/package-list.json , https://github.com/hl7-it/base
- **IT Core replaces base.** Repo `hl7-it/core`: id `hl7.fhir.it.core`, canonical `http://hl7.it/fhir/itcore`, version 0.2.0, FHIR 4.0.1, releaseLabel "trial use", publisher HL7 Italia; sushi-config has no active dependency on `hl7.fhir.eu.base` (only commented-out US Core / mCODE lines). Sources: https://github.com/hl7-it/core/blob/main/sushi-config.yaml , https://github.com/hl7-it/core
- **Ballot and publication.** HL7 Italia ran the IT CORE members' ballot 24 Apr to 18 May 2026 (announced as "versione 0.0.1 – ci-build") and announced approval and publication "in versione STU" on 25 May 2026 at https://www.hl7.it/fhir/core . The published page shows "HL7 Italia FHIR Implementation Guide (core) 0.2.0 – trial use", official URL `http://hl7.it/fhir/itcore/ImplementationGuide/hl7.fhir.it.core`, draft as of 2026-10-28, FHIR Extensions Pack 5.4.0, no EU base dependency. Sources: https://www.hl7.it/2026/04/28/al-via-il-ballottaggio-per-la-ig-fhir-it-core-di-hl7-italia/ , https://www.hl7.it/2026/05/25/approvata-e-pubblicata-la-ig-fhir-core-italia-it-core/ , https://www.hl7.it/fhir/core/index.html
- **Registry presence.** Only `hl7.fhir.it.lab-report` 0.2.0 is in the Simplifier/packages.fhir.org registry (built 2024-03-08); `hl7.fhir.it.core`, `.base`, `.terminology`, `.dossierpharma` return empty. Source: https://packages.simplifier.net/hl7.fhir.it.lab-report (checked 2026-11-18)
- **The EU dependency is in the domain IGs, not Core.** Lab Report 0.2.0 depends on `hl7.fhir.eu.base` 2.0.0, `hl7.fhir.eu.laboratory` 2.0.0, `hl7.fhir.eu.extensions.r4` 1.3.0, `hl7.fhir.it.core` 0.2.0 and `hl7.fhir.uv.ips` 1.1.0. Source: https://github.com/hl7-it/lab-report/blob/master/sushi-config.yaml

### FSE 2.0 Gateway (Ministero della Salute / Sogei / AgID)
- **Legal basis.** DM 7 Sep 2023 (GU 225, 26 Sep 2023; certifico cites GU 249 of 24 Oct 2023 for a related text) implements art. 12 c.15-quater DL 179/2012: national Gateway run by Sogei for MEF on specs from Ministero della Salute and AgID; regional EDS in each Region; FHIR R4 with Italian IGs plus CDA R2 for legacy; retroactive population from 1 Jan 2020; Allegato A lists document types; amended by decrees of 30 Dec 2024, 27 Jun 2025 and 11 Nov 2025. Sources: https://www.noze.it/en/insights/fse-2-0-gateway-sogei/ , https://www.certifico.com/news/274-news/20645-decreto-7-settembre-2023
- **Code.** Ministero della Salute GitHub org publishes the gateway microservices (AGPL v3): `it-fse-gtw-dispatcher`, `-validator`, `-fhir-mapping` (CDA to FHIR Bundle via XSLT), `-fhir-mapping-engine`, `-indexer`, `-publisher`, `-ini-client`, `-eds-client`, `-rules-manager`, `-status-manager`, `it-fse-srv-fhir`, `it-fse-srv-dictionary`, `it-fse-srv-semantic-rules-manager`, `it-fse-datarepo-fhir-server`, `it-fse-datarepo-data-quality`, `it-fse-accreditati`, `it-fse-catalogs`, `it-fse-support`. Many "srv/datarepo" repos last updated 2023-02-13; dispatcher updated 2026-11-04, support 2026-11-05, catalogs 2026-11-12. Source: https://github.com/orgs/ministero-salute/repositories
- **Integration spec.** `it-fse-support/doc/integrazione-gateway/README.md` v2.26 (30 Oct 2026) documents CDA2 validation, CDA2 publication, FHIR validation (`/v<major>/documents/fhir-validation`), and (since v2.15, 30 Jun 2025) "FHIR diretto" direct FHIR submission; v2.26 adds the CDA to FHIR transformation chapter (FHIR Mapping Language bundle generation, normalisation, deduplication, scoring, synchronous FHIR validation). CDA must be injected into a PDF as `cda.xml`. Source: https://github.com/ministero-salute/it-fse-support/blob/main/doc/integrazione-gateway/README.md
- **CDA to FHIR maps (FML) shipped by the gateway** (`it-fse-catalogs/transform`): Cartella_Clinica v1.0, Erogazione Farmaceutica v1.3 and NoSSN v1.2, Erogazione Specialistica v1.2, FULLHEADER v4.4, LetteraDimissioneOspedaliera v3.4, Lettera invito vaccinazione v1.2 / screening v1.2, Prescrizione Farmaceutica v2.4 and NoSSN v1.2, Prescrizione Specialistica v2.4, ProfiloSanitarioSintetico v2.7, Referto di Televisita v1.1, Referto di Anatomia Patologica v1.6, Referto di Radiologia v2.7, Referto Specialistica Ambulatoriale v3.5, Referto di laboratorio v2.7, Relazione Teleconsulto/Teleassistenza/Telemonitoraggio v1.1, Tessera Portatore Impianto v1.1, Vaccinazioni v2.3, Verbale Pronto Soccorso v3.0. Matching schematrons: LAB v27.1, LDO v5.5, RAD v4.1, RAP 1.4, RSA v8.3, VPS v3.8, PSS v4.0, CC v1.0, etc. Source: https://github.com/ministero-salute/it-fse-catalogs
- **Gateway terminology catalogue** (`it-fse-catalogs/terminology`, CSV per OID, `terminology.csv` 35 MB): LOINC 2.16.840.1.113883.6.1 (6.9 MB), ICD-9-CM 2.16.840.1.113883.6.103 (1.3 MB, Italian descriptions), ATC 2.16.840.1.113883.6.73, AIC drug codes 2.16.840.1.113883.2.9.6.1.5 (28 MB), CND medical device classification 2.16.840.1.113883.2.9.6.1.48, DICOM 1.2.840.10008.2.16.4, HL7 v3 vocabularies (5.x), Italian value sets 2.16.840.1.113883.2.9.77.22.11.x. Short licences for LOINC and UCUM are included. No SNOMED CT, no ICD-O, no ICD-10 file. Source: https://github.com/ministero-salute/it-fse-catalogs/tree/main/terminology
- **Status (Noze, Sep 2024):** Gateway operational, Regions connected progressively; EDS further along in Emilia-Romagna, Lombardia, Toscana, Trentino, Veneto; GP population target 85 % by June 2025; FSE 2.0 in 100 % of Regions by 2026. Source: https://www.noze.it/en/insights/fse-2-0-gateway-sogei/
- **PNRR money.** M6C2 Investment 1.3 "Rafforzamento dell'infrastruttura tecnologica e degli strumenti per la raccolta, l'elaborazione, l'analisi dei dati e la simulazione" = EUR 1.38 bn, mostly FSE 2.0 and EDS. Source: https://www.noze.it/insights/pnrr-missione6-fse-2-0/
- **Documentation portal:** https://ministero-salute.github.io/it-fse-support/ (Astro site, last updated 2024-08-06). Support channel: Slack developersitalia #fse; certificates via fse_support@sogei.it. Source: https://github.com/ministero-salute/it-fse-support

### Ecosistema Dati Sanitari (EDS)
- DM 31 Dec 2024 (GU 5 Mar 2025) institutes the EDS: Ministero della Salute data controller, Agenas processor; federated and compartmented design (identified data per Region; pseudonymised for governance and research; anonymous for epidemiology). Regional EDS harmonises to FHIR IT profiles and CDA R2 and feeds the regional FSE and national Gateway. Status 2026: "legally instituted, technically designed, PNRR-funded, not yet fully populated" (Agenda Digitale). Sources: https://www.quotidianosanita.it/governo-e-parlamento/istituita-la-cabina-di-regia-per-lattuazione-dello-spazio-europeo-sui-dati-sanitari-ehds/ , https://www.agendadigitale.eu/?p=270576 , https://www.noze.it/insights/fse-2-0-gateway-sogei/
- Umbria example of FSE 2.0 to EDS evolution (June 2025): https://insalute.regione.umbria.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Levoluzione-dal-Fascicolo-Sanitario-Elettronico-2.0-all-Ecosistema-Dati-Sanitari-in-Regione-Umbria-23-giugno-2025.pdf

## A2. Oncology FHIR in Italy

- **No Italian oncology FHIR IG exists** (HL7 Italia, Ministero, AIRTUM, ACC): confirmed again 2026-11-18 by repo listing and search. Source: https://github.com/orgs/hl7-it/repositories
- **National cancer registry data flow is XML, not FHIR.** "Piattaforma Nazionale Registri (PN REG) – Registro nazionale tumori – Specifiche funzionali dei tracciati v2.2, Jan 2025" (PNRR M6C2 sub-investment 1.3.2): four XML tracciati with XSD: A Scheda Paziente (flow RPA), B Scheda Caso (RCA), C Variabili Aggiuntive (RVA, optional, recommended for breast, colorectal, lung, melanoma, uterus), D Popolazione di Riferimento (RPR). Source: https://bura.regione.abruzzo.it/sites/bura.regione.abruzzo.it/files/bollettini/2026-09-01/allegato-4-pn-reg-registro-nazionale-tumori-specifiche-dei-tracciati.pdf (local copy italy-dl/pnreg-tracciati.pdf)

Giorgio Cangioli launched the PHOENIX European Cancer Initiative (Patient Health Oncological Expertise Network for International Exchange) at the HL7 Europe WGM Lisbon, Feb 2025. ## B. Terminologies with versions

- **ICD-9-CM 2007 (SDO)** remains the production classification for hospital discharge records and the FSE gateway dictionary (OID 2.16.840.1.113883.6.103). Source: https://github.com/ministero-salute/it-fse-catalogs/tree/main/terminology , https://www.noze.it/en/insights/snomed-loinc-icd-terminologies/
- **ICD-10-IM and CIPI (new).** DM 23 Oct 2025 (GU 286, 10 Dec 2025, ref. 25A06571) adopts ICD-10-IM (Italian modification, diagnoses) and CIPI (Classificazione Italiana delle Procedure e degli Interventi) to replace ICD-9-CM in all health information flows from 1 Jan 2027 (experimental use in 2026; bidirectional transcoding tables art. 3; "gamma 2.1" tables, final "Delta" versions v.2025 by end 2026). Ministero SDO page: experimental tracciati v1.0 Dec 2025 with ICD-10-IM fields. Sources: https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/2025/12/10/286/sg/pdf , https://www.mefop.it/cms/doc/29766/dm-salute-23-ottobre-2025.pdf , https://salute.regione.emilia-romagna.it/siseps/sanita/sdo/files/3-introduzione-icd10-im-cipi-guerzoni.pdf , https://www.salute.gov.it/new/it/sistema-informativo/scheda-dimissione-ospedaliera-sdo/
- **ICD-10 for mortality (ISTAT):** ICD-10 version 2016 from data year 2016 with the Iris automatic coding system (replacing Micar-ACME, ICD-10 2009); ICD-10 version 2019 from data year 2020; coding manual 2020 edition; Iris automatic rate about 80 %. Open tables: https://www.istat.it/tavole-di-dati/cause-di-morte/ . Sources: https://www.istat.it/it/files/2018/10/codifica-delle-cause-di-morte-ICD10-2016.pdf , https://www.istat.it/it/files/2023/04/NOTAINFORMATIVABridgeCoding2020.pdf , https://www.istat.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Rel.-finale-136.pdf
- **ICD-11:** no Italian adoption decree or official Italian translation found; the DM 23 Oct 2025 recital notes ICD-11 in force since 1 Jan 2022 and regional material says a future transition to ICD-11 is foreseen without date. Source: https://www.mefop.it/cms/doc/29766/dm-salute-23-ottobre-2025.pdf , https://salute.regione.emilia-romagna.it/siseps/sanita/sdo/documentazione/1-reminder-incontro-precedente.pdf
- **ICD-O-3 in Italy:** Italian translation of ICD-O-3 (WHO 2000) published 2005 by Inferenze Scarl, Milan, translators Adriano Giacomin (RT Biella) and Stefano Ferretti (RT Ferrara), presentation Benedetto Terracini; "Raccolta completa delle classificazioni ICD-O-3, ICD-O-3.1, ICD-O-3.2" in Italian on epiprev.it is the reference cited by the national registry spec. National registry: topography/morphology "secondo qualunque revisione della ICD-O-3 (ICD-O-3.1 o ICD-O-3.2)", field EdizioneICD-O mandatory with allowed value 3 ("in futuro 3 o 4"), CNS grade per table 27 of ICD-O-3.1. AIRTUM quality checks use ICD-O 3.2 topography/morphology compatibility rules; AIRTUM field EDICDO records edition. Sources: https://salute.regione.emilia-romagna.it/siseps/sanita/scr/files/icdo.pdf/@@download/file/icd-o.pdf , https://epiprev.it/pubblicazioni/raccolta-completa-delle-classificazioni-icd-o-3-icd-o-3-1-icd-o-3-2 , PN REG spec (above), https://www.registri-tumori.it/cms/system/files/Protocollo%20Banca%20dati%20AIRTUM%202020.pdf
- **TNM:** national registry tracciato C carries T, N, M (any edition, without prefix), "Tipo Stadio" c/p/yp/yc/I, ToS staging-system code (S, paS, ypS, cpS, coS, esS, Ti1 condensed, Ti2 essential), stage group, and mandatory "Edizione TNM utilizzata" 1-8 or 99 (UICC/AJCC manuals). AIRTUM 2020 fields STADIOT/N/M, TNM_COND (condensed TNM derived from SDO since 2018) and EDITNM. No national mandate for a specific edition; the field design admits editions 1-8 (not 9). Sources: PN REG spec https://bura.regione.abruzzo.it/sites/bura.regione.abruzzo.it/files/bollettini/2026-09-01/allegato-4-pn-reg-registro-nazionale-tumori-specifiche-dei-tracciati.pdf , AIRTUM protocol 2020 (above)
- **SNOMED CT:** Italy is not a SNOMED International member (snomed.org/members, 2026-11-18); the FSE guidelines decree of July 2022 foresaw accession, co-funded 60 % via HaDEA until 2027; no accession announced. The gateway dictionaries contain no SNOMED CT. Sources: https://www.snomed.org/members , https://www.quotidianosanita.it/?p=127694 , https://www.noze.it/en/insights/snomed-loinc-icd-terminologies/
- **LOINC Italia:** CNR (IIT-CNR, Pisa; now LOINC Italia WG) produced the first Italian version in 2010, MoU with Regenstrief 2014, biannual translation updates; UMLS lists LNC-IT-IT version 2.75 (15 Aug 2023); current Italian variant version not retrievable from loinc.org (403). DPCM 178/2015 mandates LOINC for FSE document types and lab tests; HL7 Italia Terminology IG ValueSet "sezione referto laboratorio" (active 2025-11-18); document type 11502-2. Sources: https://cdn.loinc.org/articles/?id=404 , https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/umls/sourcereleasedocs/current/LNC-IT-IT/metadata.html , https://scitepress.org/publishedPapers/2025/133808/pdf/index.html , https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-it/terminology/ValueSet-sezione-referto-laboratorio.html
- **FSE 2.0 code systems:** LOINC, ICD-9-CM, ATC, AIC (AIFA), CND devices, DICOM, HL7 v3 vocabularies, Italian value sets (gateway catalogue above). "Catalogo nazionale delle prestazioni" exists only as a draft HL7 Italia test CodeSystem `terminology.pnt.gov/CodeSystem/catalogoNazionalePrestazione` (0.0.7-beta, June 2023) plus `tariffaNazionalePrestazione` (content not-present). The new Nomenclatore of specialistica ambulatoriale (DM 23 Jun 2023, amended DM 25 Nov 2024, GU 302 of 27 Dec 2024) applies from 30 Dec 2024 and replaces the 1996 nomenclatore (3,171 services). Sources: https://packages2.fhir.org/xig/resource/eng.fhir.profile.dev%7C0.0.7-beta/CodeSystem/ba52dac7-59d6-4260-9246-0d5aaa3162cb , https://www.certifico.com/news/decreto-25-novembre-2024 , https://www.leggioggi.it/?p=91183
- **National terminology service:** n/f. `terminology.hl7.it`, `tx.hl7.it`, `terminology.pnt.gov` do not resolve (checked 2026-11-18). The Gateway distributes dictionaries as CSV/MongoDB dump in `it-fse-catalogs`; HL7 Italia Terminology IG 0.1.2 is the FHIR-published vocabulary. AgID "Catalogo delle terminologie": n/f.
- **Drug codes:** AIC (AIFA) in FHIR CodeSystem in dossier-pharma IG; ATC reference for flows. Source: https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-it/dossier-pharma/CodeSystem-aifa-aic.html
