# France: terminologies and versions in use for oncology data (status 2026-11-18)

Prepared for the editor of the HL7 Europe Common Cancer Data Model (ECCDM). Every fact carries a source URL; "n/f" = not found in any public source consulted. Facts marked **[SMT FHIR]** were read live from the ANS FHIR terminology endpoint `https://smt.esante.gouv.fr/fhir` on 2026-11-18 (anonymous access, Ontoserver 6.28.0, FHIR 4.0.1).

Given as known and not re-derived: France is a SNOMED International member since Oct 2023; the NRC is the ANS (CGTS); the French national edition is published annually on the SMT since 2024; the international edition is on the SMT; CIM-10 FR à usage PMSI (ATIH) 2026 exists; LOINC Bio FR and UCD are on the SMT; ans.fr.nos 1.1.0–1.5.0 is on Simplifier.
