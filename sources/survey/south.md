# National FHIR, oncology and terminology landscape: France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Luxembourg

Survey date: 2026-11-18. Facts only; items that could not be verified are marked "not found".

## France

1. **National core IG:** FR Core (Guide d'implémentation FR Core), package `hl7.fhir.fr.core`, canonical https://hl7.fr/ig/fhir/core, FHIR 4.0.1. Current release 2.2.0, trial-use, 2026-03-25; 3.0.0-ballot published 2026-11-09. Publisher Interop'Santé (HL7 France + IHE France); depends on the ANS nomenclature package `ans.fr.nos`. Sources: https://hl7.fr/ig/fhir/core/package-list.json , https://github.com/Interop-Sante/hl7.fhir.fr.core
2. **Oncology:** OSIRIS FHIR Implementation Guide, package `inca.fhir.uv.osiris` 1.1.0, status release (milestone, page label "trial-implementation"), 2025-04-03, FHIR 4.0.1, canonical https://ig-osiris.cancer.fr/ig/osiris. Publisher INCa; FHIR profiling by Arkhn. Implements the OSIRIS minimum data set (clinical and omics items for precision oncology), not a registry schema. Bindings are mostly SNOMED CT; the morphology value set is bound to ICD-O-3 (`http://terminology.hl7.org/CodeSystem/icd-o-3`); the TNM profile uses LOINC/UMLS codes; a CodeX radiation-therapy code system is referenced. mCODE reuse: not stated. Sources: https://ig-osiris.cancer.fr/ig/osiris/package-list.json , https://ig-osiris.cancer.fr/ig/osiris/
3. **Registry reporting:** Francim network of departmental population-based registries; incidence coded in CIM-O-3; national estimates produced by Francim, Santé publique France, INCa and HCL. Law 2025-596 of 30 June 2025 creates a national cancer registry managed by INCa; decree 2025-1366 of 26 Dec 2025 creates the processing. Transmission format: not found. Sources: https://www.vie-publique.fr/loi/289931-creation-dun-registre-national-des-cancers-loi-du-30-juin-2025 , https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/decret-n-2025-1366-du-26-decembre-2025-portant-creation-du-traitement-denomme-registre-national-des-cancers-prevu-a-larticle-l-1415-2-1-du-code-de-la-sante-publique/
4. **Terminologies:** SNOMED CT member since Oct 2023, NRC = ANS; French national edition first published on the SMT in Feb 2025 (annual); international edition on the SMT since June 2024 (https://esante.gouv.fr/produits-services/cgts/snomed-ct). CIM-10 FR à usage PMSI, ATIH, version 2026 (https://www.atih.sante.fr). CIM-O-3 in registries (sub-edition not found). LOINC: "LOINC Bio FR" on the SMT. Drugs: UCD referential on the SMT; the SMT FHIR CodeSystem endpoint (https://smt.esante.gouv.fr/fhir/CodeSystem, queried 2026-11-19) returns BDPM, CIM11_MMS and SNOMED_CT plus ANS TRE/JDV code systems; no separate CIP code system was returned (verified 2026-11-19). TNM: UICC 9th edition effective 2026-01-01 per UICC; French adoption decision not found. Terminology server: SMT (Serveur Multi-Terminologies), ANS, FHIR Terminology Service, https://smt.esante.gouv.fr/
5. **Organisations and people:** ANS (CGTS), Interop'Santé, INCa. HL7 France interim chair Nicolas Riss (ANS); technical leads François Macary, Eric Dufour, Isabelle Gibaud (https://hl7.eu/affiliate_fr.html). OSIRIS named maintainer: no maintainer page; GitHub contributors to the IG repository include an INCa account (INCaAJobard), nriss and Arkhn developers (https://api.github.com/repos/InstitutNationalduCancer/ImplementationGuide_OsirisFHIR/contributors) (verified 2026-11-19).

## Italy

1. **National base IG:** HL7 Italia FHIR Implementation Guide (base), package `hl7.fhir.it.base`, canonical http://hl7.it/fhir, FHIR 4.0.1. Last published version 0.1.0, Public Comment ballot, 2020-06-15; CI build 0.1.1 (not authorised). Publisher HL7 Italia. Sources: https://hl7.it/fhir/base/package-list.json , https://github.com/hl7-it/base . Companion: HL7 Italia Terminology IG `hl7.fhir.it.terminology` 0.1.2, active 2025-06-16 (https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-it/terminology). FSE 2.0 domain IGs (lab report 0.2.0 trial-use, dossier-pharma) are built with Ministero della Salute, AgID and Sogei.
2. **Oncology FHIR IG:** not found. No HL7 Italia oncology IG, no AIRTUM FHIR work, no Italian mCODE adoption found.
3. **Registry reporting:** AIRTUM regional/provincial registries; the AIRTUM Banca Dati protocol 2020 defines flat record layouts (tracciati A–E) with ICD-O-3 topography/morphology, ICD-10 cause of death, ICCC-3 and a TNM edition field. National Registro Nazionale Tumori at the Ministry of Health (Law 29/2019; DM 1 Aug 2023), fed by regional registries; national flow format: not found. Sources: https://www.registri-tumori.it/cms/system/files/Protocollo%20Banca%20dati%20AIRTUM%202020.pdf , https://www.normattiva.it/eli/id/2019/04/05/19G00036/ORIGINAL
4. **Terminologies:** SNOMED CT: Italy not listed on https://www.snomed.org/members (checked 2026-11-18); EU co-funding for membership exists. ICD-9-CM (Italian 2007 version) for hospital discharge records (SDO); ICD-10 for ISTAT mortality: version 2016 adopted with the Iris coder from 2016 data, version 2019 from 2020 data (https://www.ISTAT.it/it/files//2023/04/NOTAINFORMATIVABridgeCoding2020.pdf) (verified 2026-11-19); ICD-O-3.2 in AIRTUM materials; TNM 8th edition in AIRTUM training (national mandate not found). LOINC Italian translation by CNR (LOINC Italia, IIT-CNR); UMLS lists Italian edition 2.75 (Aug 2023). Drug code AIC (AIFA), FHIR CodeSystem in the dossier-pharma IG (https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-it/dossier-pharma/CodeSystem-aifa-aic.html). National terminology server: not found.
5. **Organisations and people:** HL7 Italia, Ministero della Salute, AgID, Sogei, AIRTUM. Giorgio Cangioli (HL7 Italia, author of base and terminology IGs), Mario Sicuranza, Leonardo Alcaro (terminology IG). HL7 Italia board (elected 26 June 2024): Stefano Lotti President (Chair), Giorgio Cangioli Vice-President (past chair), Leonardo Alcaro CTO, Stefano Micocci Secretary, Roberta Gazzarata Treasurer, Mario Ciampi Education (https://www.hl7.it/direttivo) (verified 2026-11-19).

## Spain

1. **National core IG:** under development; the State of FHIR 2026 summary says it is being published at unicas-fhir.sanidad.gob.es (https://packages.fhir.org/jurisdictions/ES). Existing: ÚNICAS Rare Diseases HL7 FHIR IG, package `unicas-ig`, canonical https://unicas-fhir.sanidad.gob.es, FHIR R5 (5.0.0), version 0.0.12 draft, 2026-10-13, coordinated by Ministerio de Sanidad with Cataluña leading development; depends on IPS 1.1.0. No `hl7.fhir.es.core` package found.
2. **Oncology FHIR IG:** not found. Only a 2026 "Red Nacional Interoperable" proposal (CIBERONC, VHIO, IRB Barcelona, INCLIVA, FISEVI) naming HL7 FHIR and OMOP (https://www.impacto.contraelcancer.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Red-Nacional-Interoperable.pdf). mCODE reuse: not found.
3. **Registry reporting:** REDECAN (Red Española de Registros de Cáncer) federates regional population-based registries run by the autonomous communities plus RETI; follows ENCR/IARC rules, ICD-O-3, TNM edition field 5th–8th in the REDECAN/SEOM 2023 variable set (https://seom.org//images/ESTUDIO_REGISTRO_DE_CANCER_BASE_POBLACIONAL.pdf). Submission format: not found. National framework: Real Decreto 568/2024 of 18 June creates the Red Estatal de Vigilancia en Salud Pública (BOE-A-2024-12379); a separate royal decree for a Sistema de Vigilancia del Cáncer was in public consultation (https://www.sanidad.gob.es/normativa/docs/CPP_RD_VIGILANCIA_CANCER.pdf) and not yet published (verified 2026-11-19).
4. **Terminologies:** SNOMED CT member, NRC Ministerio de Sanidad; Spanish Edition released 30 April and 31 October, SNS national extension twice yearly (https://www.sanidad.gob.es/profesionales/hcdsns/areaRecursosSem/snomed-ct/areaDescarga.htm). CIE-10-ES 6.ª edición 2026, diagnósticos and procedimientos manuals (https://www.sanidad.gob.es/estadEstudios/estadisticas/normalizacion/). CIE-O-3.2 in force 2026-01-01 (https://www.sanidad.gob.es/ca/estadEstudios/estadisticas/normalizacion/CIE10/2026/Nota_edicion_CIE_O_3_2.pdf). LOINC Spain edition 2.77, Feb 2024, Servicio Extremeño de Salud (https://loinc.org/?p=7929). Drug code (Código Nacional, AEMPS) FHIR publication: not found. TNM edition: not found. Terminology server: Servidor de Terminologías de Referencia del SNS, Ministerio de Sanidad, in production (https://www.sanidad.gob.es/areas/saludDigital/semanticaClinica/factoriaRecursos/servTerm.htm).
5. **Organisations and people:** Ministerio de Sanidad (Área de Recursos Semánticos, Unidad Técnica de Codificación), HL7 Spain (chair Francisco Pérez, https://hl7.eu/affiliate_es.html), REDECAN, SEOM. Ministry SNOMED contact in 2014–2019 documents: Arturo Romero Gutiérrez (current role not confirmed).

## Portugal

1. **National core IG:** Portuguese Core IG ("Portuguese FHIR R4B Core Profiles"), version 1.0.0, FHIR R4B, published by SPMS on Simplifier in 2024 (history entries 19-07-2024 to 29-11-2024); covers AllergyIntolerance, Bundle, MessageHeader, OperationOutcome, Parameters, Composition, List, Patient, Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Location, Organization, Schedule, Slot. Package id `portuguese.core.guide` 1.0.0, canonical base https://spms.min-saude.pt/fhir-r4B/iop, publisher SPMS (https://packages2.fhir.org/xig/portuguese.core.guide|1.0.0/StructureDefinition/AllergyIntolerancePTCore) (verified 2026-11-19). Guide: https://simplifier.net/guide/portuguese-core-ig-prod?version=1.0.0 . Separate HL7 Portugal `hl7.fhir.pt.core` 0.0.1 CI build, R4, canonical http://hl7.pt/fhir/core, generated 2024-09-23, not authorised (https://hl7-pt.github.io/core/).
2. **Oncology FHIR IG:** not found. SPMS awarded a RON platform contract on 2025-05-02 under PRR (https://op.europa.eu/en/web/public-procurement/procurement-details/-/procurement/a3e1ea2f-e50d-3f30-815e-c6a058d260d7); no FHIR specification public. mCODE: not found.
3. **Registry reporting:** RON (Registo Oncológico Nacional), Law 53/2017, single national electronic platform; all new diagnoses registered within 9 months, annual updates on stage, treatment and vital status; coordinator Maria José Bento (IPO Porto); ICD-O-3 used by RORENO. Submission format: not found (ron.min-saude.pt unreachable, no Wayback snapshot); Lei 53/2017 art. 5 defines access through a platform on the Rede Informática da Saúde with role profiles, regional registrars may extract aggregated non-identified reports (https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/53-2017-107692693) (verified 2026-11-19). Source: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/53-2017-107692693
4. **Terminologies:** SNOMED CT member since Jan 2014, SPMS Centro de Terminologias Clínicas (CTC) as NRC (https://www.snomed.org/member/portugal); national edition: not found. ICD-10-CM/PCS (English original) for hospital coding since 2017-01-01 (https://interop-pt.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CTCPT/pages/58884368). ICD-O-3. LOINC national variant: not found. Drug code CHNM (INFARMED, Portaria 155/2007, https://www.infarmed.pt/documents/15786/1068150/034-B_Port_155_2007_VF.pdf). TNM edition: not found. Terminology server: not available; SPMS PRR consultancy tender for a terminology server, 2026 (https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/anuncio-procedimento/1947-2026-1027897292).
5. **Organisations and people:** SPMS (CTC, https://www.ctc.min-saude.pt), HL7 Portugal (chair João Almeida, technical lead Ricardo Correia, https://hl7.eu/affiliate_pt.html), IPO Porto (RON coordination, Maria José Bento).

## Luxembourg

1. **National base IG:** Agence eSanté Luxembourg Simplifier organisation "esantelux" lists four guides (HPD, eResult, History API, ePrescription), all preview status, no packages (https://simplifier.net/organization/esantelux/~projects). The State of FHIR 2026 summary reports a national base IG "used in a limited set of cases", R4B/R5 in use, specifications drawing on HL7 Europe IGs and IHE profiles (https://packages.fhir.org/jurisdictions/LU). Package id/version of a base IG: not found.
2. **Oncology FHIR IG:** not found. mCODE: not found.
3. **Registry reporting:** RNC (Registre National du Cancer), established 2013 by the Direction de la santé, run by the Luxembourg Institute of Health (LIH); sources include LNS and hospital registries. Legal basis: règlement grand-ducal du 18 avril 2013 déterminant les modalités et conditions de fonctionnement du registre national du cancer (Mémorial A-79, 29 April 2013; https://gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/fr/actualites/articles/2013/05-mai/15-dibartolomeo/cancer.pdf). Coding: CIM-O-3 topography/morphology, stage per TNM 7th edition (2009), tumour groupings per ENCR-JRC definitions, in the 2020 national cancer report (https://gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/documents/actualites/2021/02-fevrier/04-journee-cancer/MSA-5632-20-rapport-cancer-2020-web-BAT-4-.pdf) (verified 2026-11-19). Submission format: not found. Source: https://researchportal.lih.lu/en/projects/registre-national-du-cancer-national-cancer-regestry/
4. **Terminologies:** SNOMED CT member since May 2018, represented by Agence eSanté (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2018/05/24/1511618/0/en/Luxembourg-Announced-as-SNOMED-International-s-Newest-Member.html); national edition: not found. Hospital coding (DCSH): ICD-10-CM diagnoses since 2016, ICD-10-PCS procedures, 2026 rules refer to the ICD-10-BE 2025 manual, APR-DRG grouping (https://santesecu.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/espace-professionnel/informations-et-donnees-de-sante/dcsh-ressources/modalits-de-codage-2026v05.pdf). ICD-O-3 (CIM-O-3) used by the RNC; TNM 7th edition in the 2020 report, later edition not confirmed (https://gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/documents/actualites/2021/02-fevrier/04-journee-cancer/MSA-5632-20-rapport-cancer-2020-web-BAT-4-.pdf) (verified 2026-11-19). LOINC variant: not found. Drug code: CNS positive list "numéro national" (https://cns.public.lu/en/legislations/textes-coordonnes/liste-positive-csv.html). Terminology server: in planning per State of FHIR 2026; an EBX-based eSanté terminology server was validated in 2016 (vendor source).
5. **Organisations and people:** Agence eSanté (https://www.esante.lu), LIH. RNC: Sophie Couffignal (operational director), Claudine Backes (scientific director), as of Oct 2023 (https://www.lih.lu/en/article/a-ten-year-milestone-the-national-cancer-registrys-decade-of-support-in-the-fight-against-cancer/). Agence eSanté CEO Alexandre Baumeister since 15 June 2026, chair of the board Marc Hostert (https://en.paperjam.lu/article/agence-esante-is-stepping-up-to-the-european-level) (verified 2026-11-19). FHIR lead: not found.
