### Sweden (SE)

Region: Nordic and Baltic. Sources: see the URLs on each statement; raw research reports in [sources/survey](https://github.com/PHOENIX-Initiative-European-Cancer/european-cancer-data-landscape/tree/main/sources/survey).

### FHIR guides, registry format and terminologies

1. **National base IG:** "Swedish Base Profiles Implementation Guide", package `hl7se.fhir.base`, FHIR R4 (4.0.1), v1.1.0 (built 2025-05-20; v2.0.0 in development), publisher HL7 Sweden, canonical http://hl7.se/fhir/ig/base. Profiles: Patient, Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Organization, HealthcareService; stated as not compatible with EU Core. E-hälsomyndigheten publishes national guides (National Medication List) on Simplifier.
   https://hl7.se/fhir/ig/base/1.1.0/ ; https://get-ig.org/hl7se.fhir.base/1.1.0 ; https://hl7europe-test.azurewebsites.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/01_eusni2025_sweden.pdf
2. **Oncology FHIR specifications:** not found. INCA (RCC platform for Cancer Registry, IPÖ, quality registers) uses an openEHR data store; no FHIR oncology IG located.
   https://svfp.se/media/biki5rbx/slutrapport_openehrv12.pdf ; https://packages.fhir.org/jurisdictions/SE
3. **Cancer registry reporting:** Cancerregistret at Socialstyrelsen; six Regional Cancer Centres receive A-reports (clinicians) and B-reports (laboratories) via INCA forms, Socialstyrelsen's form, or electronic transmission of lab diagnostic statements, code them in ICD-O/3 and deliver annually. Coding handbook "Kodning i cancerregistret 2026".
   https://cancercentrum.se/syd/regional-cancer-centres/knowledge-based-healthcare/cancer-registry/ ; https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/6b4e25c21ce446f2a9d850ef43daa294/2026-5-10247.pdf
4. **Terminologies:** SNOMED CT charter member 2007; NRC Socialstyrelsen; Swedish extension May/November, latest November 2025. ICD-10-SE 13th version for 2026 (published January 2026) plus national fördjupningskoder 2026; ICD-11 for cause-of-death from 2027. ICD-O/3.2 (2019) with Socialstyrelsen modifications. TNM: Socialstyrelsen's coding handbook 'Kodning i cancerregistret 2026' states that UICC TNM versions eight and nine are currently in use ('för närvarande används version åtta och nio') (verified 2026-11-19) https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/6b4e25c21ce446f2a9d850ef43daa294/2026-5-10247.pdf . KVÅ for procedures. LOINC, ATC: not found. Terminology servers: national FHIR terminology server in production (survey), Inera Terminologitjänst (FHIR Terminology API, fhir.inera.se); Nordic tx-nordics.fhir.org/fhir hosted by HL7 Sweden.
   https://conf.spaces.snomed.org/wiki/spaces/RMT/pages/256901154 ; https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/b4f71ee88ef74195824e5fa9cca7cf89/2026-1-9989.pdf ; https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/ad9a86523e93449097d5136a8989f561/2024-1-8918.pdf ; https://fhir.inera.se/ig/IneraFHIRTemplate/1.0.1/terminology.html

Arvid Thunholm (chair, HL7 Sweden WG base profiles); Mattias Colliander (HL7 Sweden board, terminology server); Daniel Karlsson (E-hälsomyndigheten, Nordic terminology cooperation). ### Registries, public datasets, infrastructure and non-FHIR digital assets

#### (a) Cancer registries
- Population-based: Cancerregistret at Socialstyrelsen, founded 1958; mandatory reporting; six Regional Cancer Centres (RCC) check, code and compile notifications annually. https://socialstyrelsen.se/statistik-och-data/register/cancerregistret/framstallning-och-kvalitet/ ; https://cancercentrum.se/syd/regional-cancer-centres/knowledge-based-healthcare/cancer-registry/
- Coverage: near 100% completeness (Nordic overview); Sweden lacks legal basis to use death certificates to supplement the registry. https://medicaljournalssweden.se/actaoncologica/article/view/24912
- ANCR member. https://catalogues.ema.europa.eu/network/2221158
- ENCR member registries: Swedish Cancer Registry (director Dr. David Pettersson). Institute listed as the National Board of Health and Welfare (Socialstyrelsen). Source: https://www.encr.eu/encr-members-contact-list (read 2026-11-19) (verified 2026-11-19)
- Clinical quality registries: about 30 national cancer quality registers on the INCA platform (RCC i samverkan), plus Individuell patientöversikt (IPÖ). https://cancercentrum.se/syd/regional-cancer-centres/knowledge-based-healthcare/national-swedish-cancer-registry/ ; https://akademiska.se/en/departments/specialist-healthcare/uppsala-comprehensive-cancer-centre/our-services/quality-data-at-uppsala-comprehensive-cancer-centre-uccc
- Screening registries: NKCx (Nationellt Kvalitetsregister för Cervixcancerprevention, Karolinska Institutet, 100% national coverage, annual reports at nkcx.se); mammography and colorectal screening data via INCA/RCC. https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/3a659c41107e40099b5d404ded89b50e/2020-6-6800-metodbilaga.pdf
- Submission format: A-report (clinician) and B-report (lab) via INCA forms, Socialstyrelsen form, or electronic lab diagnostic statements; RCC convert to ICD-O/3. https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/6b4e25c21ce446f2a9d850ef43daa294/2026-5-10247.pdf
- Coding: ICD-O/3.2 (applied since 2019) with Socialstyrelsen modifications; TNM mandatory since 2004, UICC TNM versions 8 and 9 currently in use per 'Kodning i cancerregistret 2026' (verified 2026-11-19) https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/6b4e25c21ce446f2a9d850ef43daa294/2026-5-10247.pdf ; ICD-10-SE. https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/ad9a86523e93449097d5136a8989f561/2024-1-8918.pdf ; https://lagen.nu/sosfs/2006:15
- Legal basis: SOSFS 2006:15 (cancer notification regulation); Patientregistret rules from 2027 mandate ICD-10-SE. https://lagen.nu/sosfs/2006:15 ; https://socialstyrelsen.se/statistik-och-data/register/patientregistret/nya-foreskrifter-om-uppgiftsskyldighet-till-patientregistret/
- Researcher access: Socialstyrelsen register service for Cancerregistret; quality registers via RCC per-register application forms and steering-group approval; ethics approval from Etikprövningsmyndigheten; SKR quick guide. https://cancercentrum.se/syd/regional-cancer-centres/research-and-innovation/registry-research/ ; https://skr.se/kvalitetsregister/omnationellakvalitetsregister/qualityregistries/registrydatainresearch/quickguideforresearchers.54757.html
