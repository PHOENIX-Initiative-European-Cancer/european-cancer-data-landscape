### Slovakia (SK)

Region: Central and Eastern Europe. Sources: see the URLs on each statement; raw research reports in [sources/survey](https://github.com/PHOENIX-Initiative-European-Cancer/european-cancer-data-landscape/tree/main/sources/survey).

### FHIR guides, registry format and terminologies

1. **National base/core FHIR IG:** none. State of FHIR 2026: no national organisation responsible for FHIR standards, no national base/core IG, no local FHIR specifications; mandatory national standard is ISO 13606 under Act 153/2013; NCZI (ezdravie) has only recently started looking at FHIR and EHDS. Only FHIR profiles with Slovak labels are in the XpanDH HDR project IG (maturity 0).
   https://api.simplifier.net/jurisdictions/SK ; https://packages.fhir.org/jurisdictions/SK ; https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-eu/xpandh-hdr/artifacts.html
2. **Oncology FHIR specifications:** none found. Only named FHIR project: Slovak BBMRI biobanking node. mCODE reuse: not found.
   https://api.simplifier.net/jurisdictions/SK
3. **Cancer registry reporting standard:** Národný onkologický register SR (NOR SR), run by NCZI (founded 1976). Since 1 Jan 2023 two reports: DGO (clinical-epidemiological) and PAT (histopathology/cytology). Submission through ISZI by (a) system-to-system link, (b) upload of an XML file generated by the provider system, or (c) manual web form; report due within 30 days. Legal basis decree 434/2024 Z. z.
   https://www.nczisk.sk/Documents/aktuality/PDF_Navod_pre_hlasenie_pacientov_do_NOR_SR.pdf ; https://nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2025/MP_ZR_NOR.pdf ; https://nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2026/MP_ZR_NOR_DGO.pdf
4. **Terminologies:** SNOMED CT: member since Nov 2009, represented by NCZI (National Health Information Center); Member Forum rep Lucia Jurovatá; national edition: not found. ICD-10: MKCH-10 (SK). ICD-O: MKCH-O-3.2 valid since 2020 (3.1 and 3.2 listed). TNM: 2024 methodology states TNM 8th edition is the valid classification (7th and 8th in code lists); the 2026 DGO guidance states TNM 9th edition takes effect 1 Jan 2026 for newly diagnosed cases, with TNM 9 code lists in Annex 1 (verified 2026-11-19) https://nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2026/MP_ZR_NOR_DGO.pdf . LOINC, ATC variant: not found. Terminology server: none and none planned.
   https://www.snomed.org/members/slovak-republic ; https://www.nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2024/Metodicky_pokyn_NOR_SR_2024.pdf ; https://nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2026/PDF_Navod_pre_hlasenie_pacientov_do_NOR_SR.pdf ; https://api.simplifier.net/jurisdictions/SK
5. **Organisations and people:** NCZI (nczisk.sk). Eva Sabajová, head of strategic planning at NCZI and HL7 Slovakia chair (Digital Health Slovakia / HL7 Slovakia affiliate).
   https://collab.hl7.at/download/attachments/108953692/03_Sabajova_NCZI_Sabajova_2024_HL7_AT.ptx.pdf?api=v2 ; https://api.simplifier.net/jurisdictions/SK

### Registries, public datasets, infrastructure and non-FHIR digital assets

#### (a) Cancer registries
- Population-based: Národný onkologický register SR (NOR SR), founded 1976, administrator NCZI; coverage 100% (ECIS); legal basis decree 434/2024 Z. z.; reports due within 30 days. https://www.nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/prezentacie/Narodny_onkologicky_register_SR.pdf ; https://nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2025/MP_ZR_NOR.pdf
- ENCR/IACR membership: n/f (ENCR contact list is script-rendered; see NEEDS BROWSER).
- Submission: ISZI system; three routes: system-to-system link, XML upload generated by provider system, manual web form; two reports DGO (clinical-epidemiological) and PAT (histopathology/cytology) since 2023. https://www.nczisk.sk/Documents/aktuality/PDF_Navod_pre_hlasenie_pacientov_do_NOR_SR.pdf
- Variable dictionary: MKCH-10; MKCH-O-3.2 valid from 2020; TNM 8th edition valid per 2024 methodology (7th and 8th in code lists); TNM 9th edition effective 1 Jan 2026 per 2026 DGO guidance, Annex 1 TNM 9 code lists (verified 2026-11-19). https://www.nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2024/Metodicky_pokyn_NOR_SR_2024.pdf ; https://nczisk.sk/Documents/nzr/2026/MP_ZR_NOR_DGO.pdf
- Clinical/hospital registries: n/f. Screening registries: NOI coordinates national screening programmes (breast, cervical, colorectal; lung/prostate in preparation). https://lpr.sk/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2025-04-07_Epidemiologicka_sprava_Svetovy_den_zdravia_final-01Apr2025_clean.docx
- Researcher access: formal procedure n/f; NCZI is central custodian, MoH Institute for Health Analyses largest secondary user (OECD). https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/health-system-performance-assessment-framework-for-the-slovak-republic_e7dbc108-en/full-report/health-data-landscape-in-the-slovak-republic-as-a-basis-for-the-hspa_c138a94a
