#### Message types and business logic
- Three bundle types distinguished by `meta.tag` from an IKNL `processing-target` code system: `NKR_ODIA` "Incidentie bericht" (patient, organisation, encounter diagnosis, problem-list conditions), `NKR_GBS` "Bericht voor verrichting" (adds EpisodeOfCare, Procedure, ServiceRequest, lab result; a `gbs-med` variant carries medication; `gbs-opt-out` carries an opt-out patient), `HOV155` (organisation plus NcrEhrDataDocument and NcrEhrSchemaDocument, i.e. the HOVON155 haemato-oncology trial dataset delivered as embedded JSON). https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/tree/develop/input/fsh/instances
- Patient selection by ICD-10: C00-C97, D01-D09, D32-D33, D35.2, D42-D43, D45-D47, E85.4, E85.8, E85.9, and all M-codes (morphology). Trigger is a new diagnosis from this list, sent 28 days after first recording to filter false-positive suspicions; daily bundles thereafter for new items (each surgical procedure; medication and radiotherapy triggers "to be added"). https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/blob/develop/input/pagecontent/Selectieenabstractieepddata.md
- Patient opt-out for research must suppress transmission, retroactively. Patients are matched across hospitals by name, postcode, birth date and tumour data. Same source.
- Transport: FHIR R4 JSON bundles over a VPN tunnel to the IKNL VPN gateway into a private IKNL FHIR server; onboarding via fhir@iknl.nl; error reports to meldingen.fhir@iknl.nl. https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/blob/develop/input/pagecontent/Requirements.md
- Disclaimer in the IG: inclusion/exclusion value sets are still under development, so filtering happens at the source; per-item value sets will be added in later versions. https://github.com/IKNL/NkrIG/blob/develop/input/pagecontent/businesslogics.md

#### Extraction architecture ("NKR-EHR" pipeline)
- R(H)ONDA (Realtime (Hemato-)ONcology Data): the automated stream went live mid-2024 from eight hospitals with a Performation "Datagateway", delivering basic patient, institution and diagnosis data (phase 1) near real-time, with eight more hospitals following; in July 2025 15 institutions were delivering; 2026 goal is to scale items and hospitals. The delivery "follows the FHIR standard". IKNL Jaarverslag 2024 p. 23 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/5e2b2905-4cf4-465c-985f-7eaa037febee/IKNL-Jaarverslag-2024.pdf ; Jaarplan 2026 p. 21.
- PLUGIN pilot with Radboudumc: the hospital exposes EHR data in FHIR, selected automatically via vantage6 (federated, data stays local); 13 items transferred successfully in the pilot; a data manager otherwise spends about 5.25 hours per patient on quality data. https://www.oncologie.nu/nieuws/plugin-pilot-iknl-haalt-realtime-data-uit-systeem-radboudumc/ ; Jaarverslag 2024 p. 23.
- Pathology feed: automated reading of PALGA protocol data since 2022 (breast protocol MammacarcinoomTotaal), 2023 three melanoma protocols plus ColonRectaalCarcinoom, 2024 cervix protocol; limits: many remaining protocols hold few NKR items, not all labs use protocols, two labs have not consented. Jaarverslag 2024 p. 14-15.
- LBZ (DHD) link: a generic person-level linkage between the Landelijke Basisregistratie Ziekenhuiszorg and the NKR was realised in 2024; the discharge date is read automatically from the LBZ; an algorithm signals metastatic breast cancer from LBZ data. Jaarplan 2026 p. 21.

### NKR (Nederlandse Kankerregistratie), IKNL
- National coverage since 1989; the only oncology hospital registry in the Netherlands with data on all cancer patients; codes TNM (UICC), ICD-O-3 topography and morphology, comorbidity at diagnosis and initial treatment; data used in more than 200 peer-reviewed publications a year; linked to IARC and ENCR, participates in EUROCARE. https://iknl.nl/en/ncr . ENCR member entry (members contact list read 2026-11-19): the Netherlands has one member, "Netherlands Cancer Registry", population 16,900,000 (year 2015), director Dr. Otto Visser. https://www.encr.eu/encr-members-contact-list (saved at scratchpad/verify/encr-members-2026-11-19.txt)
- Data sources and signalling: PALGA electronic signalling and protocol data since 2016 (protocol feed since 2022); LBZ hospital discharge data via DHD (person-level linkage realised 2024, discharge date auto-filled); Performation Datagateway from hospital EHRs (R(H)ONDA, 15 hospitals July 2025); CBS for vital status and causes of death; BRP death status under a RvIG authorisation decree. IKNL Jaarverslag 2024 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/5e2b2905-4cf4-465c-985f-7eaa037febee/IKNL-Jaarverslag-2024.pdf ; Jaarplan 2026 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/42bb9bd9-ac2b-4f95-9ed6-a6de9e830b0d/Jaarplan-2026.pdf ; BRP decree https://publicaties-prod.rvig.nl/sites/default/files/Besluit/Onderzoeksinstellingen/605301%20Integraal%20Kankercentrum%20Nederland%20%28IKNL%29.pdf
- Volumes and timeliness (Jaarplan 2026): about 200,000 tumours registered automatically from pathology in 2026 (skin cancers incl. melanoma with Breslow 0.8 mm or less since 2024) and about 130,000 partly manual; target registration within one year of diagnosis with fewer than 3,000 (2 percent) late; follow-up "vervolgepisodes" (recurrence, progression, standardised definitions since 2014) 15,000 to 20,000 a year in 2017-2023, target 25,000 in 2026 with LBZ-based signalling algorithms for at least two more tumour types; 10 to 20 externally funded additional registration projects a year with 5 to 10 FTE.
- Registration application RANK (more than ten years old) is being rebuilt from 2025 with internal and external capacity. RANK file layouts per tumour type (2025 edition) https://iknl.nl/getmedia/b7276fdb-be51-4f1d-aa3a-e20311af8c4f/RANK-Bestandsindeling-tumorsoort-2025.pdf
- Item sets per tumour type (about 45, list dated 2025-06-01) https://iknl.nl/nkr/registratie/itemsets ; per-tumour "kerndataset" plus "flexibele schil" fixed in 2025, to be finalised in 2026.
- Open data: "NKR Cijfers" interactive incidence, prevalence, survival and mortality tables https://iknl.nl/nkr-cijfers ; "NKR Online" (registered-user portal, menu item) and the "Nederlandse Kankeratlas" (with Cancer Council Queensland method) https://iknl.nl/en/ncr ; NKR data catalogue per cancer type incl. OMOP-CDM availability (Jaarplan 2026 p. 32); synthetic breast cancer dataset (also as OMOP database) https://iknl.nl/getmedia/07f1656b-5189-43ba-a62c-5daa4049170f/aanvraag-synthetische-dataset-2024.pdf ; https://github.com/IKNL/synthetic-breast-cancer-OMOP-database
- Data request procedure: only statistics or scientific research; standard deliveries exclude identifying, hospital-identifying data and exact dates; research requests need a protocol, 150-word rationale, 50-word NKR-necessity statement, funding section and per-variable justification; review by a scientific committee and the supervisory committee on the registry; download link by e-mail after approval; contact gegevensaanvraag@iknl.nl. Checklist Nov 2025 https://iknl.nl/getmedia/c11bc527-d536-4275-8b55-fb8ad3abc7c0/Checklist-bij-NKR-gegevensaanvraag-IKNL-november-2025.pdf ; infographic https://iknl.nl/getmedia/7f89d286-58f0-49ae-8c63-1e0b60932f09/IKNL-infographic-gegevensaanvraag-NKR.pdf ; scoring form https://iknl.nl/getmedia/ee18cb4c-9cdf-4d08-9485-17443785debc/Wetenschappelijke-toetsing-beoordelingsformulier-uniform.pdf
