### Greece (GR)

Region: Southern Europe. Sources: see the URLs on each statement; raw research reports in [sources/survey](https://github.com/PHOENIX-Initiative-European-Cancer/european-cancer-data-landscape/tree/main/sources/survey).

### Registries, public datasets, infrastructure and non-FHIR digital assets

#### (a) Cancer registries
- No population-based cancer registry historically (OECD cancer inequalities report). https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/beating-cancer-inequalities-in-the-eu_14fdc89a-en/full-report/component-6.html
- National Registry of Patients with Neoplastic Diseases (Εθνικό Μητρώο Ασθενών με Νεοπλασματικές Ασθένειες): created by ministerial decision 6197/2024, ΦΕΚ Β' 950/09.02.2024, under law 4600/2019 arts. 82-84; registration mandatory for all patients diagnosed after launch or treated after launch; data fields include diagnosis/histological type/grade, mode and date of diagnosis, diagnosing facility, stage at diagnosis, prognostic/predictive biomarkers; HDIKA is processor; interoperates with AMKA registry, the paediatric neoplasm registry and the national EHR (https://www.dsanet.gr/Epikairothta/Nomothesia/950.PDF) (verified 2026-11-19); in production since 24 Feb 2025; platform by IDIKA under the RRF-funded "Integrated Information System for Oncology and Haematology Patients"; states interoperability with ECIS. https://news4health.gr/politiki-ygeias/idika-enarksi-leitourgias-tou-ethnikoy-mitroou-neoplasmatikon-astheneion
- Earlier pathology-based registry 2009-2013 (>95% population). https://springerlink.bibliotecabuap.elogim.com/10.1007/s00428-017-2287-8
- Paediatric: NARECHEM-ST (University of Athens, Prof. Eleni Petridou). https://www.encr.eu/node/209
- ENCR member registries: Hellenic Cancer Registry (director Dr. Evangelia Tzala); Nationwide Registry for Childhood Hematological Malignancies (NARECHEM) (director Prof. Eleni Petridou). Corrected: the ENCR-listed paediatric registry (https://www.encr.eu/node/209) is NARECHEM for childhood haematological malignancies, not NARECHEM-ST. The Hellenic Cancer Registry entry names KEELPNO, the predecessor of EODY, so the entry predates the 2024 national registry run by HDIKA; ENCR membership of that new registry is not shown. Source: https://www.encr.eu/encr-members-contact-list (read 2026-11-19) (verified 2026-11-19)
- Greece absent from the ECIS contributing-registries list (archived) https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://ecis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/list-registries-contributing-ecis-historical-data (verified 2026-11-19). Submission format: web platform by HDIKA; ICD-O/TNM editions: not specified in the decree (n/f).
- Screening: national free preventive examinations (breast, cervical, colorectal) via e-referral on the e-health record portal. https://ehealthrecord.gov.gr/en/citizens-services
- Researcher access route: decree provides a "statistical information users" role; INAB/CERTH designated National Cancer Data Node for Greece in CANDLE (https://www.inab.certh.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESS-RELEASE-CANDLE.pdf) (verified 2026-11-19); public procedure n/f.

### National base/core FHIR IG
- HL7 Hellas publishes one guide, "HL7 FHIR GR Base and Core R5": package id hl7.fhir.gr.base-and-core-r5, canonical https://hl7-hellas.gr/fhir/hl7-fhir-gr-base-and-core-r5, FHIR 5.0.0, version 0.0.1-draft dated 2025-12-08, jurisdiction GR, licence CC0-1.0, dependency hl7.fhir.eu.base-r5 0.1.0. Source: sushi-config.yaml in https://github.com/hl7-hellas/gr-fhir-base ; guide at https://hl7-hellas.gr/fhir/hl7-fhir-gr-base-and-core-r5/ ; history https://hl7-hellas.gr/fhir/hl7-fhir-gr-base-and-core-r5/history.html
- Content: Patient base and core profiles (core imposes PatientEu and PatientEuCore; identifier slice "amka" 1..1 must-support with invariant gr-pat-core-id-amka, value.matches('[0-9]{11}')), Practitioner core (medical-licence slice, v2-0203#MD), a practitioner-specialty CodeSystem (version 0.0.1, Greek displays), logical model "Greek patient". Identifier systems: AMKA urn:oid:2.16.840.1.113883.2.25.3.4.1.1.2, medical licence urn:oid:2.16.840.1.113883.2.25.3.4.1.1.3 (input/fsh/aliases.fsh). Source: https://github.com/hl7-hellas/gr-fhir-base
- Background page references HL7 Europe Base, EPS, MPD, HDR, Lab, Imaging, IPS and xShare IPS+ as the alignment targets. Source: input/pagecontent/background.md in the repository.
- GitHub user hl7-hellas has three repositories: gr-fhir-base, gr-fhir-core (empty), hl7-hellas.github.io. Site: https://hl7-hellas.github.io/site/index.html , use cases https://hl7-hellas.github.io/site/use-cases.html
- The HL7 Hellas FHIR Work Group was established during the Athens Digital Health Week 2025 (https://www.athensdigitalhealth.eu/hl7-hellas-fhir-workgroup-established-during-the-adhw-2025/ ; https://www.athensdigitalhealth.eu/hl7-hellas-from-fhir-training-to-a-stronger-national-interoperability-roadmap/). Confluence page: https://confluence.hl7.org/spaces/HG/pages/358880751/HL7+Hellas+-+FHIR+Work+Group (not fetchable without login).
- No Greek packages on packages.simplifier.net or packages.fhir.org (catalog checked 2026-11-18).
