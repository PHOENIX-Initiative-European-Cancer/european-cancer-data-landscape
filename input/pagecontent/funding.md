### Research funding and upcoming calls

EU-level calls and running projects relevant to cancer data, interoperability and the EHDS, compiled 2026-11-18. Deadlines and budgets are as published on the survey date; check the Funding & Tenders portal before acting. National funding lines are on the country pages of Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands under "Research funding and calls".

Prepared for the PHOENIX Initiative (HL7 Europe cancer accelerator: BIH/Charité, IKNL, INCa/PSCC, Fyrstain, GHDC) and the editors of the HL7 Europe Common Cancer Data Model (ECCDM).

Sources used: the adopted Horizon Europe Work Programme 2026–2027 Part 4 Health (Decision C(2026) 6687/2 of 29 Sep 2026), Part 12 Missions and Part 3 Research Infrastructures (official PDFs on ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders), the EU4Health Work Programme 2026 annex (C(2026) 7403 final, 19 Oct 2026), the Digital Europe Work Programme 2025–2027 as amended (Oct 2025 and Mar 2026), the European Court of Auditors Special Report 07/2026, Commission policy pages and partnership call pages. Every row carries a URL; "n/f" = not found in a published source. Dates are the ones printed in the work programmes; the Funding & Tenders portal may show deadlines shifted by the Director-General (the WP allows up to two months). Where a national contact point shows a different date it is noted.

Legend for "Fit for PHOENIX": CDM = cancer data model (ECCDM/FHIR profiles), REG = registries, SYN = synthetic data, GUIDE = guideline computability, EHDS2 = EHDS secondary use. Rating: high / medium / low / none.
