### Belgium (BE)

Region: DACH and Benelux. Sources: see the URLs on each statement; raw research reports in [sources/survey](https://github.com/PHOENIX-Initiative-European-Cancer/european-cancer-data-landscape/tree/main/sources/survey).

### FHIR guides, registry format and terminologies

1. **Base IG.** eHealth Platform Federal Core Profiles / HL7 Belgium Core, package `hl7.fhir.be.core`, FHIR R4 (R4 and R4B packages), canonical https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/core/ImplementationGuide/hl7.fhir.be.core. Current published 2.2.0 (Release, STU1, package built 19 Aug 2026); previous 2.1.2 (30 Aug 2024). Publisher eHealth Platform. Transversal Clinical Core `hl7.fhir.be.core-clinical` 1.4.0 current. https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/core/2.2.0/index.html ; https://get-ig.org/hl7.fhir.be.core ; https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/core-clinical/index.html
2. **Oncology.** No published national oncology FHIR specification. "Belgian Cancer Registry on FHIR" (package `be.fhir.bcr` 0.1.0, FHIR 4.0.1, draft/experimental, dated 2026-06-03) is an individual developer's local development build by Axel Vanraes (Tiro.health) on GitHub Pages, not a published eHealth Platform or HL7 Belgium specification: its stated canonical https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/registries/bcr/ returns 404, the package is not on Simplifier or packages.fhir.org, and no hl7-be repository contains it; the copyright line names eHealth Platform Belgium only because the build uses that publisher template (verified 2026-11-19). Logical models of the BCR registration form Bijlage 55 plus three supplements (breast, MOC, follow-up), the Cancer in Belgium research dataset (metadata v2, May 2025) and cyto-histopathology screening samples; 23 CodeSystems, 33 ValueSets, all draft pending BCR review. mCODE: the npm package be.fhir.bcr 0.1.0 contains no reference to mCODE; dependencies are hl7.fhir.r4.core 4.0.1, hl7.terminology.r4 7.1.0 and hl7.fhir.uv.extensions.r4 5.2.0 (https://axelv.github.io/bcr/package.tgz) (verified 2026-11-19). https://axelv.github.io/bcr/index.html ; https://api.github.com/users/axelv . HL7 Belgium terminology IG (`hl7.fhir.be.terminology` 1.0.0) has an experimental OncologyPatientCodes value set (https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-be/terminology/en/ValueSet-OncologyPatientCodes.html).
3. **Registry standard.** Belgian Cancer Registry (Stichting Kankerregister / Fondation Registre du Cancer), Brussels. Hospitals with oncological care programmes submit via the WBCR web application or batch extraction, form Bijlage 55 of the RIZIV Verordening of 28-07-2003, amended 15-12-2025, within 4 to 6 months of incidence. healthdata.be collection BCR_Cancer (v1.1, CSV) accepts ICD-O-3 topography and morphology and official UICC TNM codes. TNM edition: not found (kankerregister.org registration pages not retrievable by automated fetch, 2026-11-19). Official batch file specification: not found (same). https://kankerregister.org/en/ ; https://axelv.github.io/bcr/StructureDefinition-bcr-cancer-registration-form.html ; https://dcd.healthdata.be/en/collection/BCR_Cancer/ ; https://www.riziv.fgov.be/SiteCollectionDocuments/formulier_verordening20030728_bijlage_55.docx
4. **Terminologies.** SNOMED CT: NRC is the Terminology Centre of FPS Public Health; first Belgian release March 2018; joined IHTSDO in 2013 according to a 2013 CliniSys presentation (secondary source, https://wiki.ihe.net/uploads/2/23/LAB_Tokyo_Semantic_Migom.pdf); listed as member by Dec 2016 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Health_Terminology_Standards_Development_Organisation) (verified 2026-11-19); snomed.org states six-monthly releases while SNOMED release notes show monthly releases since Nov 2024 (Dec 2025, Feb 2026, June 2026) (https://www.snomed.org/members/belgium ; https://conf.spaces.snomed.org/wiki/spaces/RMT/pages/476184610). ICD-10-BE (Belgian ICD-10-CM/PCS, mandatory since 2015 discharges); latest handbook found: version 2023; coding tool icd10be.health.belgium.be (https://www.health.belgium.be/sites/default/files/uploads/fields/fpshealth_theme_file/passage_a_licd-10-be_version_2023_et_au_apr-drg_version_v40.0.pdf). ICD-O-3 for registry coding (healthdata.be above). LOINC: HL7 Belgium lab IG requires LOINC per the subset defined by FPS Health (https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-be/lab/qa-ipreview.html) (verified 2026-11-19). Drug coding: SAM (Authentic Source of Medicines) with CNK product codes (NamingSystem be-ns-cnk-codes) and an ATC-to-CNK mapping published by FPS Health (https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/medication/en/NamingSystem-be-ns-cnk-codes.html ; https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/kmehr/en/tables/atc-codes) (verified 2026-11-19). Terminology server: Ontoserver 6.25.1 at https://apps.health.belgium.be/ontoserver/fhir , publisher FPS VVVL (FHIR metadata), used as authoritative SNOMED server by Belgian IGs (https://ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/core-clinical/qa-txservers.html).
5. **People.** HL7 Belgium chair Jose Costa Teixeira (https://hl7.eu/affiliate_be.html ; GitHub costateixeira); hl7-be/core top contributor Bart Decuypere (eHealth Platform) (https://github.com/hl7-be/core); BCR on FHIR author Axel Vanraes, Tiro.health, Ghent (https://api.github.com/users/axelv).

### Registries, public datasets, infrastructure and non-FHIR digital assets

#### (a) Cancer registries
- Population-based: Belgian Cancer Registry (Stichting Kankerregister / Fondation Registre du Cancer), national since 2004, mandatory registration by oncological care programmes and pathology laboratories (law of 2006); data on screening also collected; ENCR member (National Cancer Registry listed). https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2019.00120/full ; https://publications.iarc.fr/_publications/media/download/3789/c59f506329fb5e54d3a355edbb825388ed440e50.pdf
- Clinical registration: via multidisciplinary oncology consultation (MOC/COM) reimbursed under the 2003 royal decree on oncological care programmes; form Bijlage 55 (RIZIV Verordening 28-07-2003, amended 15-12-2025). https://www.riziv.fgov.be/SiteCollectionDocuments/formulier_verordening20030728_bijlage_55.docx
- Screening: BCR collects screening data; the Flemish Centrum voor Kankeropsporing (CvKO) exchanges invitation files with BCR, and BCR runs the cytohistopathology register and the Flemish cervical/HPV screening register (eHealth ISC decisions 16/019 and 12/106) (https://ehealth.fgov.be/ehealthplatform/file/cc73d96153bbd5448a56f19d925d05b1379c7f21/af05d363b492c789e44e26fd69908637761d23b7/16-019-n044-bevolkingsonderzoek_naar_dikkedarmkanker.pdf ; https://ehealth.fgov.be/ehealthplatform/file/cc73d96153bbd5448a56f19d925d05b1379c7f21/ef90e6c7022d0d953efaf93f8185d09d06734760/12-106-n354-vlaams-bevolkingsonderzoek-baarmoederhalskanker-stichting-kankerregister-gewijzigd-op-1-oktober-2024.pdf) (verified 2026-11-19); Sciensano is EUCanScreen partner. https://www.unicancer.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/eucanscreen-project-consortium-octobre-2025-1.pdf
- Submission format: WBCR web application or batch extraction; healthdata.be BCR_Cancer collection (v1.1, CSV via HD4DP) with ICD-O-3 topography/morphology, CODAP, SNOMED axes and UICC TNM; TNM edition: n/f (kankerregister.org not retrievable by automated fetch). https://dcd.healthdata.be/en/collection/BCR_Cancer/
- Data access: BCR "data request" for in-depth research; linkage via trusted third parties (eHealth TTP, CBSS); national registry number enables linkage with health insurance (IMA) and hospital discharge data (ISC deliberation 18/178, 2025). https://www.in.ehealth.fgov.be/ehealthplatform/file/cc73d96153bbd5448a56f19d925d05b1379c7f21/82f246426c189e659fa2372a0f5936dcad93ea8d/18-178-n184-herval-bij-borst--en-gynaecologische-kankerpatienten-stichting-kankerregister-gewijzigd-op-6-mei-2025.pdf

#### (b) Public datasets
- Open statistics: BCR publishes incidence, prevalence, mortality, survival (20 years of statistics, >78,000 new diagnoses); Sciensano Belgian National Burden of Disease cancer dashboard. https://kankerregister.org/en/ ; https://burden.sciensano.be/shiny/cancer/
- Research access body: Health Data Agency (HDA, law of 14 March 2023, launched 17 Jan 2024) building a national metadata catalogue; healthdata.be (Sciensano) hosts data collections. https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/creation-of-a-health-data-agency-in-belgium-a-key-agency-for-the-future-european-health-data-space ; https://dcd.healthdata.be/en/collection/BCR_Cancer/
- OMOP: BCR breast and lung dataset (incidence 2014–2023) mapped to OMOP, ETL in progress (EMA catalogue). https://catalogues.ema.europa.eu/node/4943/data-flows-and-management
- Tumour bank: Belgian Virtual Tumourbank catalogue (BVTc) coordinated by BCR, 11 university hospitals. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2019.00120/full
- ECIS contribution: the Flanders registry is in the ECIS list (58% population coverage) (https://ecis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-09/List_of_cancer_registries.pdf) (verified 2026-11-19). Synthetic datasets: n/f.
